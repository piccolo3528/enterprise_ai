## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out how Nexora employees request, book and are reimbursed for business travel and accommodation. It aims to keep travellers safe and comfortable while controlling cost.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Who is covered

This Policy applies to all Nexora employees travelling on Nexora business. Interns on approved business travel receive the entitlements of L1.

### 2.2 What is not covered

This Policy does not cover:

- relocation on transfer or joining, which is governed by a separate Relocation Policy;
- travel by candidates, consultants or contractors, which is governed by their contracts;
- daily commuting between home and the employee's base office.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Business travel | Travel on Nexora business outside the municipal limits of the employee's base city |
| Base city | The city of the employee's base office (Hyderabad, Bengaluru or Pune), as recorded in NexPeople |
| City tier | The classification of a domestic city as Tier A, Tier B or Tier C in NX-OPS-002 §4 |
| Per diem | A fixed daily allowance for meals and incidental expenses, paid without receipts |
| Travel Desk | The Finance team that books travel and accommodation through Nexora's corporate travel partner |
| International zone | The classification of a foreign country as Zone 1 or Zone 2 (Section 9.2) |

## 4. Travel Approval and Booking

### 4.1 Pre-approval

All business travel requires a travel request approved in NexClaim before any booking is made. Domestic travel is approved by the Reporting Manager. International travel is approved by the Department Head. Approval authorities are set out in NX-FIN-003 §4.

### 4.2 Booking through the Travel Desk

Flights, trains and hotels are booked by the Travel Desk after the travel request is approved. Bookings should be requested:

- at least 7 calendar days before departure for domestic flights;
- at least 21 calendar days before departure for international flights.

Requests made later than this must include a reason, which the approver confirms.

### 4.3 Self-booking

Employees may book travel or accommodation themselves only when the Travel Desk cannot be reached, such as for urgent travel outside office hours. Self-booked costs are reimbursed within the limits of this Policy, and the employee must inform the Travel Desk on the next working day.

### 4.4 Client-billable travel

Where a client contract specifies travel entitlements that are lower than this Policy, the client contract applies for travel billed to that client. Where the contract allows higher entitlements, this Policy applies.

## 5. Accommodation

### 5.1 Hotel selection

Employees should stay at hotels empanelled by the Travel Desk, which offer negotiated rates in most Tier A and Tier B cities.

### 5.2 Hotel limits per night

**Table 5.2 – Hotel limits per night (domestic, inclusive of taxes)**

| City tier | L1–L3 | L4–L6 | L7–L8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier A | ₹4,500 | ₹6,000 | ₹9,000 |
| Tier B | ₹3,500 | ₹4,500 | ₹7,000 |
| Tier C | ₹2,500 | ₹3,500 | ₹5,500 |

> **Amendment notice:** From 1 July 2026, the **Tier A row** of this table is replaced by NX-FIN-001-A1 §3.1. The Tier B and Tier C rows remain in force as shown above.

Levels are defined in NX-HR-001 §4. City tiers are defined in NX-OPS-002 §4.

### 5.3 Long stays

For stays of more than 15 consecutive nights in one city, the Travel Desk arranges serviced apartments or long-stay rates. The limit per night for a long stay is 75% of the applicable limit in Table 5.2.

### 5.4 Staying with friends or family

An employee who stays with friends or family instead of at a hotel may claim a lodging allowance of ₹1,000 per night without receipts. No hotel cost is reimbursed for those nights.

### 5.5 Exceeding the limit

Hotel costs above the applicable limit are borne by the employee unless the Department Head approved the higher rate before booking, for example because no suitable hotel was available within the limit (NX-FIN-003 §4).

### 5.6 Room sharing

Employees are not required to share rooms.

### 5.7 Laundry

Laundry costs are reimbursable only for stays of five or more consecutive nights, up to ₹500 per night.

## 6. Per Diem

### 6.1 Per diem rates

**Table 6.1 – Domestic per diem (all levels)**

| City tier | Per diem per day |
|---|---|
| Tier A | ₹1,200 |
| Tier B | ₹1,000 |
| Tier C | ₹800 |

> **Amendment notice:** From 1 July 2026, the **Tier A per diem** is replaced by NX-FIN-001-A1 §3.2. The Tier B and Tier C rates remain in force as shown above.

### 6.2 Day trips

For business travel with no overnight stay, lasting more than eight hours from departure to return, the per diem is 50% of the applicable rate in Table 6.1. No per diem is paid for trips of eight hours or less.

### 6.3 Meals provided

When all meals for a day are provided by a client, a conference or as part of a hotel package, the per diem for that day is 25% of the applicable rate.

### 6.4 What per diem covers

Per diem covers meals, beverages, tips and minor incidental expenses. It does not cover local transport (Section 8) or laundry (Section 5.7).

## 7. Mode and Class of Travel

### 7.1 Domestic travel

**Table 7.1 – Domestic travel class**

| Mode | L1–L3 | L4–L6 | L7–L8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air | Economy | Economy | Economy; flexible fare permitted |
| Rail | AC 3-tier | AC 2-tier | AC First Class |
| Bus | AC sleeper or AC seater | AC sleeper or AC seater | AC sleeper or AC seater |

### 7.2 Choice of mode

Air travel is permitted when the equivalent rail journey takes more than eight hours. For shorter journeys, rail or road travel is the default unless the approver agrees that air travel saves working time.

### 7.3 Upgrades

Upgrades to a higher class at Nexora's cost are not permitted. Upgrades using personal loyalty points are allowed.

## 8. Local Transport During Travel

App-based cabs in the economy category may be used between airports, stations, hotels, client sites and Nexora offices. Employees using their own vehicle for business travel are reimbursed at ₹12 per km for a car and ₹5 per km for a two-wheeler, plus tolls and parking. Self-drive rental cars require prior Department Head approval.

## 9. International Travel

### 9.1 Approval

International travel requires Department Head approval in NexClaim and a valid passport with at least six months' validity beyond the return date.

### 9.2 International zones

| Zone | Countries |
|---|---|
| Zone 1 | United States, United Kingdom, member states of the European Union, Switzerland, Singapore, Japan, Australia |
| Zone 2 | All other countries |

### 9.3 International limits

**Table 9.3 – International hotel limits per night and per diem (USD)**

| Zone | Hotel L1–L6 | Hotel L7–L8 | Per diem (all levels) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 | USD 180 | USD 260 | USD 75 |
| Zone 2 | USD 120 | USD 180 | USD 50 |

### 9.4 Flight class

International flights are booked in economy class for L1–L5 and premium economy for L6. Employees at L7–L8 may travel in business class on flights with a scheduled duration of more than six hours.

### 9.5 Forex, insurance and visas

- Finance issues a forex card loaded with the approved per diem and an estimate of other costs. Unused balances must be returned within 10 working days of return.
- Travel insurance is mandatory and is arranged by the Travel Desk for the business days of the trip.
- Visa fees and visa service charges are reimbursed. Passport fees are not.

## 10. Travel Advances

### 10.1 Amount

Employees may request a travel advance of up to 80% of the estimated trip cost, excluding costs booked by the Travel Desk. Advances are approved in line with NX-FIN-003 §4.

### 10.2 Settlement

Advances are settled by submitting the trip's expense claim in NexClaim within 10 working days of return. An advance not settled within 30 calendar days of return is recovered from the employee's next salary.

## 11. Combining Business and Personal Travel

### 11.1 Extending a trip

Employees may extend a business trip for personal reasons if this creates no additional cost to Nexora. Any additional flight cost is borne by the employee. Hotel costs and per diem are not paid for personal days.

### 11.2 Leave and insurance

Personal days during a trip require leave under NX-HR-002. Travel insurance arranged by Nexora covers only the business days of the trip.

### 11.3 Family members

Family members may accompany an employee at the employee's own cost.

## 12. Weekends and Long Assignments

### 12.1 Weekends

When a business trip spans a weekend, the employee may either stay at the destination, with hotel and per diem continuing, or return home and come back, whichever costs Nexora less. The approver decides if the employee wishes to choose the more expensive option.

### 12.2 Home visits on long assignments

Employees on a single assignment of more than 30 consecutive days away from their base city are entitled to one return trip home every four weeks, in the class set out in Table 7.1.

## 13. Cancellations and No-Shows

Cancellation and no-show charges caused by changes in business requirements are borne by Nexora. Charges arising from personal reasons are borne by the employee unless the Department Head waives them.

## 14. Traveller Safety

### 14.1 Travel advisories

Travel to any location subject to a government travel advisory requires approval from the Head of Operations in addition to the normal approval.

### 14.2 Traveller tracking

The Travel Desk records the location of all travellers. During a business continuity event, the Business Continuity Team uses these records to contact and support travellers under NX-OPS-001.

## 15. Claiming Travel Expenses

Travel expenses are claimed through NexClaim in accordance with the submission periods, receipt rules and approval limits in NX-FIN-002.
