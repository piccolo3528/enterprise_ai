## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out which business expenses Nexora reimburses, the limits that apply, and how employees submit, approve and settle expense claims.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all Nexora employees. Interns may claim only expenses for approved business travel and expenses their internship coordinator has approved in advance. Travel entitlements are set by NX-FIN-001 and its amendment NX-FIN-001-A1; this Policy governs how travel expenses are claimed.

## 3. General Principles

### 3.1 Conditions for reimbursement

An expense is reimbursed only if it is:

- incurred wholly for Nexora business;
- reasonable and within the limits of this Policy and NX-FIN-001;
- supported by a valid receipt, except where Section 5.2 or a per diem applies;
- claimed within the submission period in Section 4.

### 3.2 Integrity

Submitting a false, inflated or duplicate claim is fraud and a High severity violation under NX-GOV-002 §10.2.

### 3.3 Splitting

Splitting an expense into several claims, or asking colleagues to claim parts of it, to stay within a limit or an approval threshold is prohibited (NX-FIN-003 §3.4).

## 4. Submitting Claims

### 4.1 How to submit

All claims are submitted in NexClaim with receipts attached.

### 4.2 Submission periods

**Table 4.2 – Submission periods**

| Claim submitted | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Within 30 calendar days of the expense date (for travel, of the trip's return date) | Processed normally |
| 31 to 60 calendar days after | Processed only with Department Head approval of the delay |
| More than 60 calendar days after | Rejected |

### 4.3 Employees leaving Nexora

Employees leaving Nexora must submit all claims before their last working day. Under NX-HR-008 §10.2, claims submitted after the last working day are not processed, even if they fall within the periods in Table 4.2.

### 4.4 Payment

Claims fully approved by the 20th of a month are paid with that month's salary. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the following month's salary.

## 5. Receipts

### 5.1 Receipt requirement

A receipt is required for every expense of ₹500 or more. Hotel invoices must carry Nexora's GST registration number, which the Travel Desk provides.

### 5.2 Expenses below ₹500

Expenses below ₹500 may be claimed on a self-declaration without a receipt, up to a total of ₹2,000 per calendar month.

### 5.3 Lost receipts

An employee may submit a lost receipt declaration for an expense of up to ₹3,000, with Department Head approval. No more than two lost receipt declarations are accepted per employee per calendar year.

### 5.4 Foreign currency

Expenses incurred in foreign currency are converted at the rate shown on the employee's card statement or, for cash paid from a Nexora forex card, at the rate at which the card was loaded.

## 6. Approval

### 6.1 Approval limits

Claims are approved in NexClaim according to the total claim amount, under NX-FIN-003 §4:

| Claim amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹25,000 | Reporting Manager |
| ₹25,001 to ₹1,00,000 | Department Head |
| ₹1,00,001 to ₹5,00,000 | Finance Controller |
| Above ₹5,00,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

### 6.2 No self-approval

No one may approve their own claim, a claim from which they benefit, or a claim from a relative (NX-GOV-002 §5.3). A claim submitted by an approver is approved by that approver's own Reporting Manager, within the limits above.

### 6.3 Timelines

Approvers act within five working days. Claims not actioned are escalated automatically in NexClaim (NX-FIN-003 §5). Finance reviews approved claims within seven working days before payment.

## 7. Reimbursable Expenses

**Table 7.1 – Expense categories and limits**

| Category | Limit and conditions |
|---|---|
| Business travel and accommodation | As set out in NX-FIN-001 and NX-FIN-001-A1 |
| Local conveyance for business within the base city (e.g. client visits) | App-based cab, economy category, at actual cost; own car ₹12 per km; two-wheeler ₹5 per km; plus tolls and parking |
| Client entertainment | Up to ₹2,000 per person, including Nexora attendees, per occasion. Hosted by employees at L5 or above; employees at L1–L4 need Department Head pre-approval |
| Client gifts | Up to ₹3,000 per recipient per occasion (NX-GOV-002 §6.6) |
| Team meals and celebrations | Up to ₹750 per team member per quarter, with Department Head approval |
| Late working | Working in the office after 21:00 IST: dinner up to ₹400 and an economy cab home at actual cost. Not applicable to employees on night shifts who receive the cab drop in NX-HR-001 §6.5 |
| Mobile phone | Employees at L5 and above: up to ₹1,500 per month. Employees in designated on-call roles: up to ₹1,000 per month |
| Professional memberships | Up to ₹10,000 per year for a membership relevant to the role, with Department Head approval |
| Learning and certification | As set out in NX-HR-006 |
| Creche allowance (Bengaluru and Pune) | As set out in NX-HR-007 §8.2; claimed monthly with creche fee receipts |

### 7.1 Alcohol

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment hosted by an employee at L5 or above, and may not exceed 20% of the bill.

### 7.2 Remote work allowances

Internet allowances for remote work are paid through salary under the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy and may not be claimed in NexClaim.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed:

- daily commuting between home and the base office;
- traffic and parking fines;
- personal entertainment, including in-room movies and minibar items;
- costs for family members or other people accompanying the employee;
- loss or theft of personal belongings;
- tips above 10% of the bill;
- class upgrades for travel (NX-FIN-001 §7.3);
- passport fees;
- alcohol, except under Section 7.1;
- medical expenses, which are covered by Group Medical Insurance under NX-HR-001 §7.2.

## 9. Corporate Cards

### 9.1 Eligibility

Corporate cards are issued to employees at L6 and above, and to other employees who travel on business at least six times a year, with Department Head approval.

### 9.2 Monthly limits

| Cardholder | Monthly limit |
|---|---|
| L6 and frequent travellers below L6 | ₹1,50,000 |
| L7–L8 | ₹3,00,000 |

### 9.3 Use and reconciliation

Corporate cards may be used only for business expenses. Each month's transactions must be reconciled in NexClaim, with receipts, within 15 calendar days of the statement date. Personal use, even if repaid, results in withdrawal of the card and may be treated as a violation under NX-GOV-002.

### 9.4 Card transactions and approval

Reconciled card transactions follow the same approval limits as claims (Section 6.1).

## 10. Advances

Travel advances are requested and settled as set out in NX-FIN-001 §10.

## 11. Audit and Recovery

### 11.1 Audit

Finance audits a random sample of at least 10% of approved claims each month, in addition to all claims above ₹1,00,000.

### 11.2 Recovery

Amounts found to have been reimbursed in error are recovered from the employee's next salary after the employee is notified. Suspected fraud is referred to the Ethics Committee under NX-GOV-002 §13.
