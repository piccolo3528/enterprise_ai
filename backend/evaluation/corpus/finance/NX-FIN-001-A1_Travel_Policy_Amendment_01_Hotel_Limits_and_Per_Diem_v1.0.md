## 1. Purpose

Hotel rates and meal costs in Tier A cities have risen since NX-FIN-001 version 2.0 took effect on 1 April 2025. Travellers at L1–L6 have increasingly been unable to find suitable empanelled hotels within the limits. This Amendment raises the Tier A hotel limits for L1–L6 and the Tier A per diem. It makes no other change to NX-FIN-001.

## 2. Base Document and Precedence

This Amendment amends **NX-FIN-001 version 2.0**. Under NX-GOV-001 §9.1 (rule 3), this Amendment prevails over NX-FIN-001 only for the clauses listed in Section 3. Every other clause of NX-FIN-001 remains in force exactly as written. The version number of NX-FIN-001 does not change.

## 3. Changes to NX-FIN-001

### 3.1 Replacement of the Tier A row of Table 5.2 (hotel limits per night)

**Type of change:** Replacement of one table row. The Tier B and Tier C rows of Table 5.2 are not changed.

The Tier A row of NX-FIN-001 Table 5.2 is replaced in full by the following row:

**Table 3.1 – Replacement Tier A row (domestic hotel limits per night, inclusive of taxes)**

| City tier | L1–L3 | L4–L6 | L7–L8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier A | ₹5,500 | ₹7,500 | ₹9,000 (unchanged) |

The previous Tier A limits of ₹4,500 (L1–L3) and ₹6,000 (L4–L6) cease to apply from the effective date. The L7–L8 limit of ₹9,000 is restated without change for completeness.

### 3.2 Replacement of the Tier A value in Table 6.1 (per diem)

**Type of change:** Replacement of one value. The Tier B and Tier C per diem rates are not changed.

The Tier A per diem in NX-FIN-001 Table 6.1 is replaced: **₹1,200 per day is replaced by ₹1,500 per day**, for all levels.

### 3.3 Addition of Section 5.8 (peak-period rates in Tier A cities)

**Type of change:** New clause added to NX-FIN-001 Section 5.

The following clause is added as NX-FIN-001 §5.8:

> **5.8 Peak-period rates in Tier A cities.** During major trade fairs, conferences or events that raise hotel prices across a Tier A city, the Department Head may approve, before booking, a hotel rate of up to 25% above the applicable Tier A limit. This approval is given under the authority to approve hotel costs above the limit in NX-FIN-003 §4. Rates more than 25% above the limit are not permitted.

## 4. Provisions That Remain Unchanged

For the avoidance of doubt, the following provisions of NX-FIN-001 are **not** changed by this Amendment:

- the Tier B and Tier C rows of Table 5.2;
- the Tier B and Tier C per diem rates in Table 6.1;
- Section 5.3 (long stays) — the 75% long-stay limit is now calculated on the amended Tier A limits;
- Section 6.2 (day trips) and Section 6.3 (meals provided) — the 50% and 25% reductions are now calculated on the amended Tier A per diem;
- Sections 7 to 15, including all international limits in Table 9.3;
- the city tier classification, which remains as set out in NX-OPS-002 §4.

## 5. Application to Travel in Progress

### 5.1 Date of stay, not date of claim

The amended limits apply to hotel nights and per diem days falling **on or after 1 July 2026**. Hotel nights and per diem days before 1 July 2026 are reimbursed at the previous limits, even if the claim is submitted or approved after 1 July 2026 (NX-GOV-001 §7.4).

### 5.2 Trips spanning the effective date

For a trip that spans 1 July 2026, each night and each day is assessed separately.

*Example:* An L2 Software Engineer stays in Mumbai (Tier A) from the night of 28 June 2026 to the morning of 3 July 2026. The nights of 28, 29 and 30 June are subject to the ₹4,500 limit. The nights of 1 and 2 July are subject to the ₹5,500 limit. The per diem is ₹1,200 for 28–30 June and ₹1,500 for 1–3 July.

## 6. Worked Examples (from 1 July 2026)

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| L5 Manager, 4 nights in a Tier A city, August 2026 | Hotel limit ₹7,500 per night; per diem ₹1,500 per day |
| L2 Software Engineer, 20 consecutive nights in a Tier A city, September 2026 | Long stay: 75% × ₹5,500 = ₹4,125 per night |
| L3 Senior Analyst, day trip of 10 hours to a Tier A city, October 2026 | 50% × ₹1,500 = ₹750 |
| L4 Lead, 3 nights in a Tier B city, August 2026 | Unchanged: hotel limit ₹4,500 per night; per diem ₹1,000 per day |
| L7 Director, 2 nights in a Tier A city, September 2026 | Unchanged: hotel limit ₹9,000 per night; per diem ₹1,500 per day |
| L4 Lead, Tier A city during a major trade fair, DH approval obtained before booking | Up to 125% × ₹7,500 = ₹9,375 per night |
