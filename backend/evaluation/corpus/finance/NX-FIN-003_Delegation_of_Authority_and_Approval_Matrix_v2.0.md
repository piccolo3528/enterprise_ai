## 1. Purpose

This document defines who may approve what at Nexora. It is the canonical source for approval authority roles and financial and non-financial approval limits (NX-GOV-001 §10). Where another policy names an approver, it should match this document; if it does not, this document prevails (NX-GOV-001 §9.1, rule 4).

## 2. Scope

This document applies to all approvals given in NexPeople, NexClaim, NexLearn, NexDesk and the procurement system, and to any written approval given outside those systems.

## 3. Approval Authority Roles and Principles

### 3.1 Roles

**Table 3.1 – Approval authority roles**

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Reporting Manager (RM) | The employee's line manager as recorded in NexPeople |
| Department Head (DH) | The head of one of Nexora's six departments, normally at L6 or L7 |
| HR Business Partner (HRBP) | The Human Resources partner assigned to the employee's department |
| Head of HR | The head of the Human Resources department |
| Finance Controller | The senior finance manager responsible for accounting and payments, at L6 or above |
| Head of IT Security | The head of the information security function within the IT department |
| Head of Operations | The head of the Operations department; also leads the Business Continuity Team |
| Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | The executive responsible for finance |
| Chief Executive Officer (CEO) | The executive head of Nexora |

### 3.2 No self-approval

No one may approve a request they submitted, a request from which they personally benefit, or a request concerning a relative or close personal relationship (NX-GOV-002 §5.3). Such requests go to the approver's own RM, or to the next authority in the relevant table.

### 3.3 Sequential approval

Where a row lists more than one approver, approvals are obtained in the order listed. For example, "RM + DH" means the RM approves first and the DH second.

### 3.4 No splitting

Splitting a transaction, claim or purchase into smaller parts to bring each below an approval limit is prohibited and is treated as a violation of NX-GOV-002 §10.

### 3.5 Delegation during absence

An approver may delegate their approval authority in NexPeople or NexClaim for a period of up to 30 calendar days to an employee at the same level or higher. Delegation to an employee at a lower level is not permitted. The CFO and CEO may delegate only to each other. The delegating approver remains accountable for approvals given in their name.

## 4. Approval Matrix

### 4.1 Expenses and travel

**Table 4.1 – Expenses and travel**

| Item | Approver |
|---|---|
| Expense claim up to ₹25,000 | RM |
| Expense claim ₹25,001 to ₹1,00,000 | DH |
| Expense claim ₹1,00,001 to ₹5,00,000 | Finance Controller |
| Expense claim above ₹5,00,000 | CFO |
| Claim submitted 31–60 days after the expense (NX-FIN-002 §4.2) | DH, in addition to the amount-based approver |
| Lost receipt declaration (NX-FIN-002 §5.3) | DH |
| Domestic travel request | RM |
| International travel request | DH |
| Hotel rate above the applicable limit (NX-FIN-001 §5.5) | DH, before booking |
| Travel to a location under a government travel advisory | Head of Operations, in addition to the travel approver |
| Travel advance up to ₹50,000 | RM |
| Travel advance above ₹50,000 | DH |
| Client entertainment hosted by an employee at L1–L4 | DH, before the event |
| Team meals and celebrations | DH |
| Professional membership | DH |
| Corporate card issue to an employee below L6 | DH |
| Travel or accommodation paid for by a client or vendor (NX-GOV-002 §6.5) | DH |

### 4.2 People and HR

**Table 4.2 – People and HR**

| Item | Approver |
|---|---|
| Leave of up to 10 consecutive working days | RM |
| Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days | RM + DH |

| Leave Without Pay of 31 to 90 calendar days in a leave year | DH + Head of HR |
| Probation extension (NX-HR-001 §5.3) | RM, with HRBP concurrence |
| Promotion to L2, L3 or L4 | DH |
| Promotion to L5 or L6 | DH + Head of HR |
| Promotion to L7 or L8 | CEO |
| Off-cycle promotion (NX-HR-005 §8.5) | Head of HR, in addition to the approver above |
| Work from anywhere (NX-HR-003 §5) | RM |
| Fully remote arrangement (NX-HR-003 §6) | DH + Head of HR |
| Outside employment (NX-HR-001 §9) | DH + HRBP |
| Waiver of notice shortfall of up to 30 days (NX-HR-008 §6.3) | DH |
| Waiver of notice shortfall of more than 30 days | DH + Head of HR |
| Garden leave (NX-HR-008 §6.5) | DH, with HRBP |
| New position within the approved headcount budget | DH |
| New position outside the approved headcount budget | CFO |
| New position at L7 or L8 | CEO |

### 4.3 Learning and development

**Table 4.3 – Learning and development**

| Certification not on the approved list (NX-HR-006 §5.1) | DH |

### 4.5 IT and information security

**Table 4.5 – IT and information security**

| Item | Approver |

| Exception allowing USB storage devices | Head of IT Security |

### 4.6 Business continuity

**Table 4.6 – Business continuity**

| Item | Approver |
|---|---|
| Declaring a Sev-2 incident and activating the Business Continuity Plan (NX-OPS-001) | Head of Operations |
| Declaring a Sev-1 incident | CEO, on the recommendation of the Head of Operations |
| Office closure or mandatory remote working for one office | Head of Operations |
| Office closure or mandatory remote working for all offices | CEO |
| Recalling employees from Annual Leave (NX-HR-002 §5.5) | CEO, only during a declared Sev-1 incident |

### 4.7 Policy exceptions

**Table 4.7 – Policy exceptions (NX-GOV-001 §12)**

| Item | Approver |
|---|---|
| Exception to a Finance policy (FIN documents) | CFO |
| Exception to an IT policy (IT documents) | Head of IT Security |
| Exception to an HR policy where the policy names no exception authority | DH + Head of HR |
| Exception to any other policy where the policy names no exception authority | DH + Head of HR |
| Exceptions that cannot be granted | Applicable law; NX-GOV-002 §6; mandatory IT Security requirements (NX-GOV-001 §12.4) |

## 5. Escalation of Pending Approvals

An approval not actioned within five working days is escalated automatically to the next authority in the relevant table, or to the approver's own RM where no higher authority is listed. Leave requests follow the three-working-day escalation in NX-HR-002 §5.4.

## 6. Records and Audit

All approvals are recorded in the relevant system with the approver's identity and date. Approvals given outside a system, such as by email, must be uploaded to the relevant record within two working days. Finance's internal audit reviews compliance with this document each quarter and reports findings to the CFO.
