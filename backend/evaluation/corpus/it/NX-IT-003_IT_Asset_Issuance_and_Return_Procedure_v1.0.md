## 1. Purpose

This Procedure sets out which IT equipment Nexora issues to employees, how it is issued, how employees must look after it, what happens if it is lost or damaged, and how it is returned.

## 2. Scope

This Procedure applies to all IT equipment owned or leased by Nexora and issued to employees, interns and contractors. Client-issued equipment is covered only by Section 9.

## 3. Standard Equipment

**Table 3.1 – Standard equipment by role**

| Role | Equipment issued |
|---|---|
| Engineering roles | Laptop (32 GB memory), charger, headset, laptop bag |
| Business roles (HR, Finance, Sales, Operations, IT administration) | Laptop (16 GB memory), charger, headset, laptop bag |
| Designated on-call roles and employees at L7–L8 | Mobile phone, in addition to the above |
| Employees approved to work remotely on a full-time basis | One external monitor for home use, on request |

Monitors, keyboards and docking stations at office desks are shared office equipment and are not issued to individuals.

## 4. Issuance

### 4.1 New joiners

The Reporting Manager raises a laptop and access request in NexDesk at least 10 working days before the new joiner's date of joining (NX-HR-004 §3.4). Requests raised later are fulfilled as soon as possible, and a loaner laptop may be issued in the meantime.

### 4.2 Handover and acknowledgement

IT hands over equipment in person on the employee's first day, or ships it to employees who join remotely. The employee confirms receipt by signing the asset acknowledgement in NexDesk, which records the asset tag and serial number of each item.

### 4.3 Refresh

Laptops are replaced every four years from the date of issue, or earlier if IT determines that a laptop can no longer be repaired economically.

### 4.4 Additional equipment

Requests for equipment beyond Table 3.1 require Department Head approval in NexDesk and a business justification.

## 5. Care of Equipment

Employees must:

- use Nexora equipment only for Nexora work and the limited personal use permitted by NX-IT-001 §4.2;
- not allow anyone else, including family members, to use it;
- not remove or cover asset tags, or open or modify the hardware;
- follow the physical security rules in NX-IT-001 §6.6.

## 6. Loss, Theft and Damage

### 6.1 Reporting loss or theft

Loss or theft of any Nexora device must be reported to IT Security within one hour of discovery, as a security incident under NX-IT-001 §12. IT Security remotely locks and wipes the device.

### 6.2 Police report

For theft, the employee must file a police report within 24 hours and upload a copy to the NexDesk incident within three working days.

### 6.3 Cost of loss or theft

| Situation | Cost to the employee |
|---|---|
| First loss or theft within 24 months, reported on time and, for theft, with a police report | None |
| Loss caused by negligence, such as leaving the device unattended in a public place or in a vehicle | Recovery value (Section 8) |
| Second or later loss or theft within 24 months | Recovery value (Section 8) |
| Loss or theft not reported within the required time | Recovery value (Section 8) |

### 6.4 Accidental damage

The first accidental damage repair within 24 months is free of charge. For any further accidental damage within 24 months, the employee pays 50% of the repair cost, up to a maximum of ₹15,000 per incident. Deliberate damage is charged at the full repair cost or the recovery value, whichever is lower, and may be treated as a violation under NX-GOV-002.

## 7. Return of Equipment

### 7.1 When equipment must be returned

All issued equipment must be returned:

- by the last working day when leaving Nexora (NX-HR-008 §9.1);
- within five working days of a replacement device being issued;
- when requested by IT for repair or audit.

### 7.2 How to return

Employees working from an office hand equipment to the IT counter at their base office. Employees working remotely request a courier collection in NexDesk at least five working days before their last working day; Nexora pays for the courier.

### 7.3 Return receipt

IT inspects returned equipment and issues an Asset Return Receipt in NexDesk, which confirms clearance for the IT section of the exit clearance workflow (NX-HR-008 §10).

### 7.4 Data handover and device wipe

IT wipes a returned laptop only after the Reporting Manager confirms in NexDesk that the employee's data handover is complete (NX-IT-002 §10). Employees must not wipe or reset devices themselves.

### 7.5 Transfers between offices

When an employee transfers to another Nexora office, their issued equipment transfers with them and the asset record is updated in NexDesk.

### 7.6 No sale of devices

Nexora does not sell issued devices to employees, including departing employees.

## 8. Recovery Value

Where this Procedure or NX-HR-008 §9.3 requires the value of equipment to be recovered, the following values apply.

**Table 8.1 – Recovery values**

| Item | Recovery value |
|---|---|
| Laptop or mobile phone | Purchase cost reduced by 25% for each completed year since the date of issue, subject to a minimum of 20% of the purchase cost |
| Charger | ₹2,500 |
| Headset | ₹3,000 |
| Laptop bag | ₹1,500 |
| External monitor (home use) | ₹8,000 |
| Access badge | ₹500 |

*Example 1:* A laptop bought for ₹1,20,000 was issued 2 years and 4 months ago. Two completed years reduce its value by 50%, so the recovery value is ₹60,000.

*Example 2:* The same laptop issued 4 years and 2 months ago would be reduced by 100%, but the minimum of 20% applies, so the recovery value is ₹24,000.

A lost access badge during employment is replaced at the badge recovery value of ₹500.

## 9. Client-Issued Equipment

Equipment issued by a client is governed by the client's terms. Employees must return it through Nexora's IT counter, which returns it to the client. Any charge a client makes to Nexora for lost or damaged client equipment may be recovered from the employee on the same basis as Section 6.

## 10. Disposal

Retired equipment is disposed of through a certified e-waste vendor. Storage is securely erased before disposal, and the vendor provides a data destruction certificate for each device.
