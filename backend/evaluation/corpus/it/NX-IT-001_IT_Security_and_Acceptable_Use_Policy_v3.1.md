## 1. Purpose

This Policy protects Nexora's and its clients' systems and information. It sets out how Nexora IT resources may be used, the security controls every user must follow, how to report security incidents and how to get IT support.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all employees, interns, contractors and consultants who use Nexora devices, accounts, networks or systems, wherever they work.

## 3. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head of IT Security | Owns this Policy; approves exceptions and new software; leads security incident response |
| IT Service Desk (NexDesk) | First point of contact for IT requests and incident reports |
| Users | Follow this Policy; protect credentials and devices; report incidents promptly |
| Reporting Managers | Ensure their team members complete security training and request only the access they need |

## 4. Acceptable Use

### 4.1 Business use

Nexora IT resources are provided for business purposes.

### 4.2 Limited personal use

Limited personal use, such as reading personal email or browsing during breaks, is permitted if it does not interfere with work, consume significant network capacity or breach any other rule in this Policy.

### 4.3 Prohibited use

Users must not:

- access, store or share illegal, offensive or sexually explicit material;
- run cryptocurrency mining, peer-to-peer file sharing or torrent software;
- attempt to disable, bypass or test security controls, unless authorised in writing by the Head of IT Security;
- install software not in the approved catalogue (Section 8);
- share passwords or MFA codes, or let anyone else use their account;
- store Nexora or client information in personal email, personal cloud storage or personal devices, except as permitted in Section 6.4;
- use Nexora resources to run a personal business.

## 5. Accounts and Authentication

### 5.1 Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the user's last five passwords. Passwords are changed every 180 days, or immediately if a compromise is suspected.

### 5.2 Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the approved authenticator app is mandatory for all Nexora systems. SMS codes are used only for account recovery. New joiners set up MFA on their first day (NX-HR-004 §4.2).

### 5.3 Lockout

An account is locked for 15 minutes after five consecutive failed sign-in attempts.

### 5.4 Shared and privileged accounts

Shared accounts are not permitted. Users who need administrative access to systems receive a separate privileged account, which must not be used for email or browsing.

## 6. Devices

### 6.1 Nexora-issued devices

Only Nexora-issued laptops may be used for Nexora work. Devices are issued and returned under NX-IT-003. All laptops have full-disk encryption, endpoint detection and response (EDR) software and automatic updates. Security updates must be installed within 14 days of release; devices more than 14 days behind lose access to internal systems until updated.

### 6.2 Screen lock

Screens lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Users must lock their screen whenever they leave their device.

### 6.3 Local administrator rights

Local administrator rights are not granted by default. A user who needs them raises a NexDesk request, which requires approval from their Department Head and the Head of IT Security. Approved rights are granted for a maximum of 90 days and may be renewed.

### 6.4 Personal mobile devices

Personal mobile phones and tablets may be used to access Nexora email, calendar and collaboration tools only through the approved mobile management app, which keeps Nexora data in a separate encrypted container. Nexora information must never be stored on personal laptops or desktops.

### 6.5 Removable storage

USB storage devices, external hard drives and memory cards are blocked on all Nexora laptops. The Head of IT Security may approve an exception for a specific business need, for a maximum of 30 days.

### 6.6 Physical security

Laptops must not be left unattended in public places or in vehicles. Employees take their laptops home at the end of each working day unless they use an assigned locker, so that they can work remotely if an office becomes unavailable (NX-OPS-001).

## 7. Networks and Remote Access

### 7.1 VPN

Internal systems can be reached from outside a Nexora office only through the Nexora VPN.

### 7.2 Public and home networks

Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected. Home routers used for Nexora work must use WPA2 or stronger encryption, and their default administrator password must be changed. Mobile phone hotspots are permitted.

### 7.3 Work from anywhere

When working from anywhere under NX-HR-003 §5, users must follow this Section and must not access information classified Restricted from public or shared networks (NX-IT-002).

## 8. Software and Cloud Services

### 8.1 Approved catalogue

Users may install only software listed in the approved catalogue in NexDesk. Requests for new software or cloud services are reviewed by IT Security within 10 working days and approved by the Head of IT Security.

### 8.2 Generative AI tools

Only generative AI tools on the approved list in NexDesk may be used for Nexora work. Nexora's approved enterprise AI assistant is configured so that prompts are not used to train external models. The classes of information that may be entered into AI tools are set out in NX-IT-002 §5.

### 8.3 Client-mandated tools

Where a client requires the use of specific tools, these may be used within environments the client provides, under the client's security terms.

## 9. Email and Communication

### 9.1 Phishing

Users must report suspected phishing messages using the "Report phishing" button in their email client and must not click links or open attachments in suspicious messages.

### 9.2 External forwarding

Automatic forwarding of Nexora email to external addresses is blocked.

### 9.3 External email banner

Emails from outside Nexora carry a banner reading "External sender". Requests for payments, credentials or changes to bank details from external senders must be verified by phone using a known number.

## 10. Monitoring

Nexora monitors the use of its devices, networks and systems to protect security and comply with law. Security logs are retained for 12 months. Users should have no expectation of privacy in their use of Nexora systems, subject to applicable law.

## 11. Security Training

### 11.1 New joiners

New employees must complete Information Security Awareness training in NexLearn within seven calendar days of joining. Access to client systems is not granted until it is completed (NX-HR-004 §5.1).

### 11.2 Annual refresher

All users complete the annual refresher by 30 June each year.

### 11.3 Phishing simulations

IT Security runs phishing simulations every quarter. A user who fails two simulations within 12 months must repeat Information Security Awareness training within 14 calendar days.

## 12. Reporting Security Incidents

### 12.1 What to report

Users must report any actual or suspected security incident, including:

- a lost or stolen device, access badge or security token;
- a suspected compromised account or password;
- information sent to the wrong recipient;
- malware or unusual device behaviour;
- unauthorised access to information or premises.

### 12.2 How and when to report

Incidents must be reported within one hour of discovery, by raising a "Security Incident" request in NexDesk, emailing security@nexora-tech.example or calling internal extension 7100. Lost or stolen devices are also handled under NX-IT-003 §6.

### 12.3 What not to do

Users must not investigate an incident themselves, delete evidence or contact any suspected attacker.

### 12.4 Severity

IT Security assigns each confirmed incident a severity under NX-OPS-001 §4. Incidents assessed as Sev-1 or Sev-2 are escalated to the Business Continuity Team.

## 13. IT Support

### 13.1 Support tiers

**Table 13.1 – NexDesk support tiers**

| Tier | Team | Handles |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | IT Service Desk | First contact; password resets; access requests; basic troubleshooting. Available 08:00–22:00 IST on working days, and 24 hours a day for P1 tickets |
| Tier 2 | Desktop and Application Support | Device faults, software installation, application issues escalated by Tier 1 |
| Tier 3 | Infrastructure, Network and Security Engineering | Server, network, cloud and security issues escalated by Tier 2 |

### 13.2 Ticket priorities

**Table 13.2 – Ticket priorities and targets**

| Priority | Definition | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Many users cannot work, a critical system is down, or a security incident is reported | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | One user cannot work, or a team's work is seriously affected, with no workaround | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 | Work is affected but a workaround exists | 4 working hours | 3 working days |
| P4 | Service requests and minor issues | 1 working day | 10 working days |

Ticket priority is separate from the incident severity levels in NX-OPS-001 §4. Every incident declared Sev-1 or Sev-2 is handled as a P1 ticket.

## 14. Violations

Breaches of this Policy may lead to suspension of access and disciplinary action under NX-GOV-002 §13. Deliberate circumvention of security controls is treated as a High severity violation.

## 15. Exceptions

Exceptions to this Policy are approved by the Head of IT Security under NX-GOV-001 §12. The following requirements are mandatory and no exception may be granted: MFA (Section 5.2), full-disk encryption and EDR (Section 6.1), and incident reporting (Section 12).
