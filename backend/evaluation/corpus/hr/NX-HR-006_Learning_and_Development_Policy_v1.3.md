## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out how Nexora supports employees' learning: the annual learning target, learning budgets, certification sponsorship, exam leave and the conditions attached to sponsored learning.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility

### 2.1 Employees covered

This Policy applies to full-time permanent employees at all levels, including employees on probation.

### 2.2 Fixed-term contract employees

Fixed-term contract employees may use the learning budget only for programmes that are completed before their contract end date and that relate directly to their current project. They are not eligible for sponsorship above ₹50,000.

### 2.3 Employees not covered

Interns and contractors are not covered by this Policy. Interns may access NexLearn courses assigned by the internship coordinator.

## 3. Annual Learning Target

### 3.1 Target

Every employee is expected to complete at least 40 learning hours in each performance cycle (1 April to 31 March). Progress is considered under the Growth competency in NX-HR-005 §6.2.

### 3.2 What counts towards the target

**Table 3.2 – Learning hours**

| Activity | Hours credited |
|---|---|
| NexLearn courses | Course duration as listed in NexLearn |
| External courses and workshops | Actual hours attended, on upload of a completion certificate |
| Conferences | Actual hours attended, up to 16 hours per conference |
| Certification exam passed | 10 hours per certification, in addition to preparation courses |
| Delivering an internal Nexora Academy session | Twice the session length |
| Mandatory compliance training | Up to 8 hours per cycle |

### 3.3 Recording

Learning outside NexLearn must be recorded in NexLearn, with a certificate or attendance proof, within 30 calendar days of completion. Learning recorded after 30 days does not count towards the target.

## 4. Annual Learning Budget

### 4.1 Budget by level

**Table 4.1 – Annual learning budget**

| Level (NX-HR-001 §4) | Budget per financial year (1 April – 31 March) |
|---|---|
| L1–L3 | ₹30,000 |
| L4–L6 | ₹50,000 |
| L7–L8 | ₹75,000 |

### 4.2 What the budget covers

The budget covers course and workshop fees, certification exam fees, conference registration fees, and books and online subscriptions of up to ₹5,000 per year within the budget.

### 4.3 What the budget does not cover

Travel and accommodation for training or conferences are not paid from the learning budget. Where the Department Head approves attendance, travel is booked and reimbursed under NX-FIN-001 and NX-FIN-002.

### 4.4 Use of the budget

The budget is personal to the employee and may not be transferred to another employee. Unused budget lapses on 31 March and is not carried forward or paid out.

### 4.5 Employees on probation

Employees on probation may use up to 50% of their annual budget. The remainder becomes available on confirmation.

### 4.6 Joiners and level changes

Employees joining during the financial year receive a pro-rata budget for the remaining full months. An employee promoted on 1 July receives the budget for the new level from that date, pro-rata for the remaining months, in addition to any unused budget at the previous level.

## 5. Certification Sponsorship

### 5.1 Approved certifications

L&D maintains a list of approved certifications in NexLearn, covering cloud platforms, enterprise software, security, data and project management. A certification not on the list may be sponsored if the Department Head confirms it is relevant to the employee's current or planned role.

### 5.2 Pre-approval

Employees must obtain approval in NexLearn before enrolling or booking an exam. Fees paid without prior approval are not reimbursed.

### 5.3 Exam fee reimbursement

Nexora reimburses the exam fee for a certification passed on the first or second attempt. Fees for failed attempts are not reimbursed. No fee is reimbursed for a third or later attempt.

*Example:* An employee fails an exam costing ₹15,000 and passes on the second attempt. Nexora reimburses ₹15,000 for the passed attempt only.

### 5.4 Claims

Sponsored fees are either paid directly by L&D or claimed by the employee through NexClaim within 30 calendar days of the result, following NX-FIN-002.

### 5.5 Certifications held at the time of leaving

Certifications earned with Nexora sponsorship belong to the employee, subject to the service commitment in Section 6.

## 6. Service Commitment for Sponsored Learning

### 6.1 When a service commitment applies

Sponsorship of more than ₹50,000 for a single programme or certification carries a service commitment of 12 months from the date the programme is completed. The employee signs a sponsorship agreement in NexLearn before enrolment.

### 6.2 Recovery

If the employee resigns before the end of the 12-month commitment, Nexora recovers a pro-rata share of the sponsorship amount for the unserved months. The amount is recovered in the full and final settlement under NX-HR-008.

*Example:* Nexora sponsors a ₹90,000 programme completed on 1 May 2026. The employee's last working day is 31 August 2026, four months later. Eight months of the commitment remain unserved, so Nexora recovers 8/12 × ₹90,000 = ₹60,000.

### 6.3 No recovery

No amount is recovered if employment ends because of redundancy, the death or permanent disability of the employee, or termination by Nexora for reasons other than misconduct.

### 6.4 Programmes above the annual budget

A programme costing more than the employee's annual budget may be sponsored only with the joint approval of the Department Head and the Head of HR. The full cost is charged against the employee's budget for the year, and any excess is funded centrally by L&D.

### 6.5 Employees on a PIP

Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan are not eligible for sponsorship above ₹50,000 (NX-HR-005 §9.5).

## 7. Exam Leave

Employees receive one day of paid exam leave for each sponsored certification exam, up to a maximum of four days per financial year. Exam leave is applied for in NexPeople under the "Exam Leave" type and does not reduce any balance under NX-HR-002.

## 8. Mandatory Training

Mandatory training is assigned in NexLearn. Deadlines are set by the governing policies.

**Table 8.1 – Mandatory training**

| Training | Frequency | Governing document |
|---|---|---|
| Code of Conduct | On joining; annual refresher by 31 March | NX-GOV-002 §14 |
| Information Security Awareness | On joining; annual refresher | NX-IT-001 |
| Information Classification and Data Handling | On joining; every two years | NX-IT-002 |
| Prevention of Sexual Harassment | On joining; annual refresher | NX-GOV-002 §4.2 |

Completion of mandatory training affects the maximum performance rating under NX-HR-005 §4.3.

## 9. Requesting External Training

1. Identify the course, certification or conference and its cost.
2. Submit a request in NexLearn at least 15 working days before the start date.
3. The request is approved in line with NX-FIN-003 §4: by the Reporting Manager for amounts up to ₹50,000 within the employee's budget, and by the Department Head for amounts above ₹50,000.
4. L&D pays the provider directly, or the employee pays and claims the cost under Section 5.4.
5. Upload the completion certificate or result in NexLearn within 30 calendar days of completion.

## 10. Nexora Academy

Nexora Academy is Nexora's internal learning programme. Employees at L3 and above may propose and deliver sessions on technical or professional topics. Sessions are scheduled by L&D and recorded in NexLearn. Presenters receive learning hours as set out in Table 3.2.
