## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out the leave available to Nexora employees, how leave is credited and applied for, and how unused leave is carried forward, lapses or is encashed. It is the canonical source for the leave year, leave types and leave entitlements (NX-GOV-001 §10).

## 2. Scope and Eligibility

### 2.1 Employees covered

This Policy applies to full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees at all levels and all locations.

### 2.2 Employees not covered

Interns receive leave as stated in their internship letter. Contractors and consultants are not covered.

### 2.3 Parental leave

Maternity, paternity, adoption and surrogacy leave are governed by NX-HR-007, which prevails over this Policy on those subjects (NX-GOV-001 §9.1, rule 5).

## 3. Leave Year and Leave Types

### 3.1 Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

### 3.2 Leave types

**Table 3.2 – Leave types**

| Leave type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Annual Leave (AL) | Planned time off for rest, holidays and personal matters. Replaces "Earned Leave" used in version 2.0. |
| Sick Leave (SL) | Absence due to the employee's own illness or injury, or medical appointments |
| Casual Leave (CL) | Short, unplanned absences for personal matters |
| Compensatory Off | Time off in lieu of approved work on a weekend or public holiday (Section 7) |
| Restricted Holiday (RH) | Optional holidays chosen from the list in NX-OPS-002 §6 (Section 8) |
| Bereavement Leave | Death of a family member |
| Marriage Leave | The employee's own marriage |
| Leave Without Pay (LWP) | Unpaid leave after relevant paid leave is exhausted |

## 4. Entitlements

### 4.1 Annual entitlement

**Table 4.1 – Entitlement per leave year**

| Leave type | Entitlement | Credit method |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Leave | 20 working days | Accrues at 1.67 days per month, credited on the first day of each month |
| Sick Leave | 8 working days | Credited in full on 1 January |
| Casual Leave | 6 working days | Credited in full on 1 January |
| Restricted Holiday | 2 days | Credited on 1 January |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 working days (immediate family); 2 working days (grandparents) | Per occasion, on application |
| Marriage Leave | 5 working days | Once during employment |
| Leave Without Pay | Up to 90 calendar days per leave year | Section 4.6 |

### 4.2 New joiners

- Annual Leave accrues from the month of joining if the employee joins on or before the 15th of that month, and from the following month otherwise.
- Sick Leave and Casual Leave are credited pro-rata for the remaining months of the leave year, rounded to the nearest half day.
- Employees joining after 30 June receive one Restricted Holiday instead of two.

*Example:* An employee joining on 10 July is credited 8 × 6/12 = 4 days of SL and 6 × 6/12 = 3 days of CL, accrues AL from July, and receives one Restricted Holiday.

### 4.3 Leave during probation

Employees on probation may use the AL they have accrued and their full credited SL and CL. They may not take Marriage Leave until they are confirmed.

### 4.4 Negative balances

Leave may not be taken in excess of the available balance. A confirmed employee may take up to three days of AL in advance of accrual with HRBP approval. Advance leave not yet earned on the last working day is recovered in the full and final settlement (NX-HR-008).

### 4.5 Bereavement Leave

Immediate family means spouse, child, parent, sibling and parent-in-law. Bereavement Leave must begin within 14 calendar days of the death.

### 4.6 Leave Without Pay

LWP may be taken only after the employee's AL and CL balances are exhausted, or SL balance for absence due to illness. LWP of up to 30 calendar days in a leave year requires Department Head approval. LWP of more than 30 and up to 90 calendar days also requires approval of the Head of HR. LWP beyond 90 calendar days in a leave year is not permitted under this Policy.

### 4.7 Effect of LWP on accrual

AL does not accrue for any calendar month in which the employee takes more than 10 working days of LWP.

## 5. Applying for Leave

### 5.1 How to apply

All leave is applied for and approved in NexPeople.

### 5.2 Notice requirements

**Table 5.2 – Leave application notice**

| Leave | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| AL of 5 or more consecutive working days | 14 calendar days before the first day of leave |
| AL of fewer than 5 working days | 2 working days |
| CL | Inform the Reporting Manager before 10:00 IST on the day; apply in NexPeople the same day |
| SL | Inform the Reporting Manager before 10:00 IST on the day; apply in NexPeople within 2 working days of returning |
| Restricted Holiday | 7 calendar days |
| Marriage Leave | 30 calendar days |

A Reporting Manager may accept shorter notice for AL in genuine emergencies.

### 5.3 Approval authority

The Reporting Manager approves leave of up to 10 consecutive working days. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days also requires the approval of the Department Head, in line with NX-FIN-003 §4.

### 5.4 Response time

The approver must approve or reject a leave request within three working days. Requests not actioned within this period are escalated automatically in NexPeople to the next approver. They are not approved automatically.

### 5.5 Rejection and cancellation

An approver who rejects a request must record a reason. An employee may cancel approved leave in NexPeople before it begins. Nexora may recall an employee from approved AL only for a declared Sev-1 incident under NX-OPS-001; recalled days are re-credited.

### 5.6 Casual Leave limits

CL may not be taken for more than three consecutive working days and may not be combined with AL in a single continuous absence.

### 5.7 Medical certificate

A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for SL of more than two consecutive working days.

### 5.8 Half days

AL, SL and CL may be taken in half-day units.

### 5.9 Weekends and public holidays

Weekends and public holidays at the employee's office location (NX-OPS-002 §6) that fall within a period of AL are not counted as leave.

*Example:* AL from Thursday to the following Tuesday, with a public holiday on Monday, counts as three days of AL (Thursday, Friday and Tuesday).

## 6. Carry-Forward, Lapse and Encashment

### 6.1 Carry-forward

Up to 10 days of unused AL may be carried forward from one leave year to the next. AL above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

### 6.2 Leave that does not carry forward

SL, CL, Restricted Holidays and Compensatory Off are not carried forward and lapse on 31 December, or earlier where Section 7 says so.

### 6.3 No year-end encashment

Unused AL is not encashed at the end of the leave year. Year-end encashment under version 2.0 ended with the encashment for the 2025 leave year (Section 10.3).

### 6.4 Encashment at exit

On leaving Nexora, unused AL of up to 30 days is encashed at the daily rate of basic salary (monthly basic salary divided by 30). AL above 30 days lapses. The calculation is made as part of the full and final settlement under NX-HR-008. SL, CL, Restricted Holidays and Compensatory Off are not encashed at exit.

## 7. Compensatory Off

### 7.1 Earning Compensatory Off

An employee earns Compensatory Off when they work on a weekend or public holiday with the prior written approval of their Reporting Manager:

- 4 to 8 hours of work earns a half day;
- more than 8 hours earns a full day.

Work of less than 4 hours does not earn Compensatory Off.

### 7.2 Using Compensatory Off

Compensatory Off must be used within 60 calendar days of the day worked, after which it lapses.

### 7.3 Eligibility

Employees at L6, L7 and L8 are not eligible for Compensatory Off.

## 8. Restricted Holidays

### 8.1 Choosing Restricted Holidays

Each employee may take up to two Restricted Holidays per leave year, chosen from the list for their office location in NX-OPS-002 §6. Restricted Holidays not on that list cannot be taken.

### 8.2 Application

Restricted Holidays must be applied for at least seven calendar days in advance. A Reporting Manager may decline a Restricted Holiday only where more than half the team has already been approved for the same date.

### 8.3 Lapse

Unused Restricted Holidays lapse at the end of the leave year and are not encashed.

## 9. Leave in Special Circumstances

### 9.1 Leave during the notice period

Leave during the notice period is governed by NX-HR-008.

### 9.2 Remote work is not leave

A day worked remotely under NX-HR-003 is a working day, not leave. An employee who is unwell must apply for SL rather than record a remote working day.

### 9.3 Illness during Annual Leave

If an employee is hospitalised for three or more consecutive days during AL, the affected days may be converted to SL on submission of a hospital discharge summary within 7 calendar days of returning to work.

### 9.4 Office closures

Days on which an office is closed by a business continuity declaration under NX-OPS-001 are not deducted from any leave balance, unless the declaration requires employees to work remotely.

## 10. Transition from Version 2.0

### 10.1 Carried-forward balances

Earned Leave carried forward into the 2026 leave year under version 2.0 (up to 15 days) is retained in full and converted to AL on 1 April 2026. The 10-day carry-forward limit in Section 6.1 applies for the first time on 31 December 2026.

*Example:* An employee carried 15 days of Earned Leave into 2026. All 15 days remain available as AL. On 31 December 2026, she may carry forward a maximum of 10 days of her remaining AL into 2027.

### 10.2 Annual Leave accrual in 2026

Accrual for January to March 2026 was 1.5 days per month under version 2.0. Accrual from April 2026 is 1.67 days per month. An employee employed for the whole of 2026 therefore accrues 19.5 days of leave for 2026 (4.5 days for January–March plus 15 days for April–December). From 2027, the full entitlement of 20 days applies.

### 10.3 Year-end encashment for 2025

The year-end encashment of up to 10 days for the 2025 leave year was made under version 2.0 with the January 2026 salary. No year-end encashment applies for 2026 or any later year.

### 10.4 Casual/Sick Leave in 2026

Unused combined Casual/Sick Leave credited on 1 January 2026 under version 2.0 is converted to SL on 1 April 2026, up to a maximum of 4 days; any excess lapses. From 1 April 2026, employees are additionally credited with 6 days of SL and 4.5 days of CL for the remainder of 2026.

### 10.5 Approved leave

Leave applied for and approved before 1 April 2026 for dates on or after 1 April 2026 stands as approved, even if it did not meet the notice requirements of this version.

### 10.6 Compensatory Off earned before 1 April 2026

Compensatory Off earned before 1 April 2026 may be used within 90 days of the day worked, as provided in version 2.0.
