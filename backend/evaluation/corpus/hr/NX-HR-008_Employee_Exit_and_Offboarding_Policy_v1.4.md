## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out how employment with Nexora ends: notice periods, the resignation process, handover, asset return, clearance, full and final settlement and post-employment obligations. It is the canonical source for notice periods (NX-GOV-001 §10).

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees at all levels. Interns follow the terms of their internship letter, except that Sections 8 and 9 (handover and asset return) apply to them in full.

## 3. Types of Separation

| Type | Initiated by |
|---|---|
| Resignation | Employee |
| Termination for performance (after a PIP under NX-HR-005 §9) | Nexora |
| Termination for misconduct (after an Ethics Committee finding under NX-GOV-002 §13) | Nexora |
| Redundancy | Nexora |
| End of a fixed-term contract | Contract expiry |
| Retirement at age 60 | Automatic |
| Death or permanent incapacity | — |

## 4. Notice Periods

### 4.1 Notice period table

**Table 4.1 – Notice periods**

| Employment status and level | Notice period |
|---|---|
| On probation (all levels) | 15 calendar days |
| Confirmed, L1–L3 | 60 calendar days |
| Confirmed, L4–L6 | 90 calendar days |
| Confirmed, L7–L8 | 120 calendar days |

### 4.2 Application

The notice period applies equally to resignation by the employee and to termination with notice by Nexora. It is based on the employee's level and confirmation status on the date notice is given.

*Example:* An L3 Senior Software Engineer promoted to L4 on 1 July who resigns on 10 July serves 90 days, because she is at L4 on the date of resignation.

### 4.3 Start of the notice period

The notice period starts on the day after the employee submits their resignation in NexPeople. The last working day (LWD) is calculated automatically and shown in NexPeople.

### 4.4 Fixed-term contracts

A fixed-term contract employee follows Table 4.1, but the notice period cannot extend beyond the contract end date. No notice is required for a contract ending on its scheduled end date.

### 4.5 Performance Improvement Plans

An employee on a PIP is a confirmed employee and serves the notice period for their level (NX-HR-005 §9.5).

## 5. Resignation Process

1. The employee discusses their intention with their Reporting Manager (RM).
2. The employee submits their resignation in **NexPeople → Exit → Resign**, stating the reason.
3. The RM acknowledges the resignation within two working days.
4. The RM may hold a retention discussion within five working days. A resignation may be withdrawn only with the RM's and Department Head's (DH) agreement, before the LWD.
5. The RM and HRBP confirm the LWD and any early release (Section 6) in NexPeople.

## 6. Early Release, Notice Shortfall and Garden Leave

### 6.1 Early release at the employee's request

An employee may request to leave before the end of their notice period. The RM and DH decide whether to agree, taking account of handover needs.

### 6.2 Notice shortfall recovery

Where early release is agreed, the unserved notice days are recovered at the daily rate of basic salary (monthly basic salary divided by 30), unless the recovery is waived.

### 6.3 Waiver of shortfall

A waiver of notice shortfall is approved in line with NX-FIN-003 §4: by the DH for a shortfall of up to 30 days, and by the DH together with the Head of HR for a shortfall of more than 30 days.

*Example:* An L5 Manager with a 90-day notice period is released after 45 days. The 45-day shortfall exceeds 30 days, so a waiver needs both the DH and the Head of HR. Without a waiver, 45 days of basic salary are recovered.

### 6.4 Pay in lieu of notice by Nexora

Nexora may end employment before the end of the notice period by paying basic salary for the unserved notice days.

### 6.5 Garden leave

Nexora may place an employee on garden leave for all or part of the notice period. During garden leave, the employee:

- receives full salary and benefits;
- performs no work and is not required to attend the office;
- must remain available to answer handover questions by phone or email;
- may not start work with another employer before the LWD;
- has access to Nexora systems suspended, except NexPeople.

Garden leave is decided by the DH with the HRBP and is commonly used for employees joining a competitor or client.

## 7. Leave During the Notice Period

### 7.1 Annual Leave

Annual Leave during the notice period requires approval from both the RM and the DH. Annual Leave cannot be used to bring the LWD forward; the LWD changes only through early release under Section 6.

### 7.2 Sick Leave

Sick Leave may be taken during the notice period under NX-HR-002, with a medical certificate for any absence of more than two consecutive working days.

### 7.3 Unauthorised absence

Unapproved absence during the notice period extends the LWD by the number of days absent, unless the DH agrees to treat the days as Leave Without Pay.

### 7.4 Leave encashment

Unused Annual Leave is encashed in the full and final settlement under NX-HR-002 §6.4, which caps encashment at 30 days at the daily rate of basic salary.

## 8. Knowledge Transfer and Data Handover

### 8.1 Handover plan

The employee and RM agree a written handover plan in NexPeople within five working days of the resignation being acknowledged. The plan lists open work, documents, client contacts and recurring responsibilities.

### 8.2 Data handover

Before the LWD, the employee transfers all Nexora and client information in their custody to a custodian nominated by the RM, in accordance with NX-IT-002. Information classified Confidential or Restricted must be transferred, not deleted, unless the information owner approves deletion.

### 8.3 Personal copies

The employee must not keep, copy, forward to personal accounts or delete any Nexora or client information. Doing so is a breach of NX-GOV-002 §7.

## 9. Asset Return and Access Revocation

### 9.1 Asset return

All Nexora assets, including laptops, accessories, access badges and any client-issued equipment, must be returned by the LWD in accordance with NX-IT-003.

### 9.2 Access revocation

Access to Nexora systems ends at 18:00 IST on the LWD. Access is suspended immediately when:

- the employee is placed on garden leave (Section 6.5);
- employment is terminated for misconduct;
- IT Security identifies a risk under NX-IT-001.

### 9.3 Unreturned assets

The value of any asset not returned, or returned damaged beyond normal wear, is recovered from the full and final settlement at the value set out in NX-IT-003.

## 10. Exit Clearance

### 10.1 Clearance workflow

Clearance is completed through the Exit Clearance workflow in NexPeople. Each department confirms clearance by the LWD.

**Table 10.1 – Clearance checklist**

| Department | Confirms |
|---|---|
| IT | Assets returned; data handed over; accounts scheduled for revocation |
| Finance | Outstanding advances settled; all expense claims submitted |
| Facilities (Operations) | Access badge, locker and parking pass returned |
| Human Resources | Handover plan completed; exit interview offered; recoveries calculated |

### 10.2 Expense claims at exit

All pending expense claims must be submitted in NexClaim before the LWD. Claims submitted after the LWD are not processed, notwithstanding the submission periods in NX-FIN-002.

## 11. Full and Final Settlement

### 11.1 Timing

The full and final settlement (F&F) is paid within 45 calendar days of the LWD, provided clearance is complete.

### 11.2 Components

**Table 11.2 – F&F components**

| Payable to the employee | Recoverable from the employee |
|---|---|
| Salary up to the LWD | Notice shortfall not waived (Section 6.2) |
| Annual Leave encashment, up to 30 days (NX-HR-002 §6.4) | Annual Leave taken in advance and not earned (NX-HR-002 §4.4) |
| Approved, unpaid expense reimbursements | Pro-rata learning sponsorship (NX-HR-006 §6.2) |
| Gratuity, where applicable under law after five years of continuous service | Value of unreturned assets (NX-IT-003) |
| — | Outstanding salary or travel advances |

### 11.3 Grievances

The F&F is not withheld while a grievance is pending (NX-GOV-003 §7.5).

### 11.4 Death in service

In the event of an employee's death, the F&F is paid to the nominee registered in NexPeople within 30 calendar days of receipt of the required documents. No recoveries are made except outstanding advances. The HRBP assists the family with insurance claims under NX-HR-001 §7.2.

## 12. Exit Interview

The HRBP offers every departing employee a confidential exit interview during their last 10 working days. Participation is voluntary. Feedback is reported to the Head of HR in aggregate form only.

## 13. Exit Documents

| Document | Issued |
|---|---|
| Relieving letter | On the LWD, if clearance is complete; otherwise within 5 working days of clearance |
| Experience letter | With the F&F |
| Salary and tax statements | With the F&F |

## 14. Termination by Nexora

### 14.1 Performance

Employment terminated after an unsuccessful PIP ends with notice under Table 4.1 or pay in lieu of notice.

### 14.2 Misconduct

Where the Ethics Committee finds a High severity violation under NX-GOV-002 §13.3, employment may be terminated without notice or pay in lieu of notice.

### 14.3 Redundancy

Employees whose roles are made redundant receive notice under Table 4.1 (or pay in lieu) and severance of 15 days' basic salary for each completed year of service, with a minimum of one month's basic salary.

## 15. Re-hire Eligibility

Former employees are eligible for re-hire unless they were terminated for a High severity violation, or left without serving their notice period or obtaining a waiver. Probation on re-hire follows NX-HR-001 §5.6.

## 16. Post-Employment Obligations

### 16.1 Confidentiality

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends (NX-GOV-002 §7.3).

### 16.2 Non-solicitation

For 12 months after the LWD, former employees must not solicit Nexora employees to leave Nexora, or solicit Nexora clients they worked with during their last 12 months of employment to move work away from Nexora.
