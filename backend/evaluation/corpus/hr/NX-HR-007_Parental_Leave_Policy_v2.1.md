## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out the leave and support Nexora provides to employees when a child is born, adopted or received through surrogacy. On these subjects, it prevails over the Annual Leave and Leave Management Policy (NX-HR-002) under NX-GOV-001 §9.1, rule 5. Where applicable law provides a greater entitlement than this Policy, the law prevails.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility

### 2.1 Employees covered

This Policy applies to full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees at all levels and all locations, including employees on probation.

### 2.2 Fixed-term contract employees

For fixed-term contract employees, parental leave ends no later than the end date of the fixed-term contract, unless the contract is renewed.

### 2.3 Employees not covered

Interns and contractors are not covered by this Policy.

## 3. Types of Parental Leave

**Table 3.1 – Parental leave entitlements**

| Leave type | Entitlement | Eligibility condition |
|---|---|---|
| Maternity Leave – first and second child | 26 weeks | At least 80 days worked at Nexora in the 12 months before the expected date of delivery |
| Maternity Leave – third and subsequent child | 12 weeks | As above |
| Adoption Leave (adoptive mother) | 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over | Child below three months of age at adoption |
| Surrogacy Leave (commissioning mother) | 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over | — |
| Paternity Leave | 10 working days | Birth of the employee's child, or adoption of a child below one year of age |
| Miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy | 6 weeks from the date of the event | Medical certificate required |

All parental leave is fully paid at the employee's normal salary.

## 4. Maternity Leave

### 4.1 Timing

Up to eight weeks of Maternity Leave may be taken before the expected date of delivery. The remainder is taken after the delivery.

### 4.2 Notification

The employee should inform her Reporting Manager and HRBP, and apply in NexPeople, at least eight weeks before she intends to start Maternity Leave. A medical certificate confirming the expected date of delivery must be uploaded with the application.

### 4.3 Employees who do not meet the 80-day condition

An employee who has worked fewer than 80 days at Nexora in the 12 months before the expected date of delivery is not entitled to paid Maternity Leave under this Policy. She may use her available leave under NX-HR-002 and up to 90 calendar days of Leave Without Pay, which the Head of HR approves without requiring the balance-exhaustion condition in NX-HR-002 §4.6 to be met.

### 4.4 Complications

Where a medical practitioner certifies complications arising from pregnancy, delivery or premature birth, the employee is entitled to up to one additional month of paid leave.

### 4.5 Extending Maternity Leave

An employee may extend Maternity Leave by using her AL balance, followed by Leave Without Pay of up to three months with Department Head approval.

## 5. Paternity Leave

### 5.1 Timing

Paternity Leave must be taken within three months of the child's birth or the date of adoption. It may be taken in a single block or in a maximum of two blocks.

### 5.2 Application

Paternity Leave should be applied for in NexPeople at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave where the date is known. For births earlier than expected, the employee applies within two working days of the birth.

### 5.3 Lapse

Paternity Leave not taken within three months lapses and is not encashed.

## 6. Adoption and Surrogacy Leave

### 6.1 Documentation

Adoption Leave and Surrogacy Leave require a copy of the adoption order, or the surrogacy agreement and the hospital's handover document, uploaded in NexPeople.

### 6.2 Adoption of older children

Adoptive mothers who adopt a child aged three months or older are not entitled to Adoption Leave under Table 3.1. They may take up to 15 working days of special leave with pay, approved by the HRBP, within one month of the adoption.

## 7. Return to Work

### 7.1 Remote work after Maternity Leave

On returning from Maternity, Adoption or Surrogacy Leave, an employee may work remotely on a full-time basis for up to 12 weeks, with the agreement of her Reporting Manager. During this period, the minimum office attendance requirement in NX-HR-003 §4 does not apply.

### 7.2 Role protection

An employee returning from parental leave returns to the same role, or to an equivalent role at the same level, location and pay.

### 7.3 Phased return

An employee returning from Maternity Leave may request a phased return of up to four weeks, working six hours a day at full pay, approved by the Department Head.

## 8. Childcare Support

### 8.1 Hyderabad

The Hyderabad office has an on-site creche for children aged six months to six years, available free of charge to employees based at that office (see NX-OPS-002).

### 8.2 Bengaluru and Pune

Employees based in the Bengaluru and Pune offices may claim a creche allowance of ₹5,000 per month per family until the child reaches six years of age, on submission of creche fee receipts through NexClaim.

### 8.3 Both parents employed by Nexora

Where both parents are Nexora employees, only one parent may claim the creche allowance, and only one may enrol the child in the on-site creche.

## 9. Interaction with Other Policies

### 9.1 Annual Leave accrual

AL continues to accrue under NX-HR-002 during paid parental leave. It does not accrue during Leave Without Pay used to extend parental leave, where NX-HR-002 §4.7 applies.

### 9.2 Public holidays

Public holidays and weekends falling within Maternity, Adoption, Surrogacy or miscarriage leave are counted as part of the leave period, because these entitlements are expressed in weeks. Public holidays falling within Paternity Leave are not counted, because Paternity Leave is expressed in working days.

### 9.3 Performance reviews

An employee's performance rating may not be lowered because of parental leave. An employee absent on parental leave for more than half of a review cycle receives the rating treatment set out in NX-HR-005.

### 9.4 Medical insurance

Maternity expenses are covered under Group Medical Insurance (NX-HR-001 §7.2) up to ₹50,000 for a normal delivery and ₹75,000 for a caesarean delivery. Newborns are covered from birth, provided they are enrolled in NexPeople within 30 calendar days of birth.

### 9.5 Probation

Probation is extended by the length of any parental leave of more than four weeks taken during probation. This extension does not count towards the single extension limit in NX-HR-001 §5.3.
