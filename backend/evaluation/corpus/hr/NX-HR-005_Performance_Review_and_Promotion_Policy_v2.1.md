## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out how Nexora sets goals, reviews performance, assigns ratings, promotes employees and supports employees whose performance falls below expectations. It is the canonical source for the performance rating scale (NX-GOV-001 §10).

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees at L1 to L7. The performance of employees at L8 is reviewed by the CEO under the same rating scale, outside the calibration process in Section 5.4. Interns are assessed under their internship letter.

## 3. Job Level Framework

*Removed in version 2.1.* The job level framework is maintained in NX-HR-001 §4.

## 4. Rating Scale

### 4.1 Ratings

**Table 4.1 – Performance rating scale**

| Rating | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | Consistently far exceeds goals; impact extends well beyond own role and team |
| 4 | Exceeds | Exceeds most goals; regularly contributes beyond the role |
| 3 | Meets | Achieves goals and demonstrates the expected competencies for the level |
| 2 | Partially Meets | Achieves some goals; specific improvement is needed in identified areas |
| 1 | Does Not Meet | Does not achieve most goals; significant and immediate improvement is required |

### 4.2 Distribution guideline

Calibration aims for no more than 10% of a department's rated employees at Outstanding and no more than 25% at Exceeds. These percentages are guidelines for calibration, not quotas, and a department may exceed them with the Head of HR's approval.

### 4.3 Rating limits

An employee's final rating cannot be higher than Meets if, at the end of the cycle, either of the following applies:

- the employee has not completed mandatory compliance training by its deadline (NX-GOV-002 §14, NX-IT-001);
- the employee received a Medium or High severity finding under NX-GOV-002 §13.3 during the cycle.

## 5. Review Cycle

### 5.1 Cycle period

The performance cycle runs from 1 April to 31 March.

### 5.2 Timeline

**Table 5.2 – Annual review timeline**

| Activity | Timing |
|---|---|
| Goal setting for the new cycle | By 30 April (new joiners: within 30 days of joining) |
| Mid-year check-in | 1–31 October |
| Self-assessment for the cycle just ended | 1–10 April |
| Manager assessment | 11–25 April |
| Department calibration | 1–20 May |
| Final ratings released in NexPeople | By 31 May |
| Promotions take effect | 1 July |

### 5.3 Goals

Each employee agrees between four and eight goals with their Reporting Manager (RM) in NexPeople. Goals may be revised at the mid-year check-in or when the employee changes role.

### 5.4 Calibration

Department calibration is chaired by the Department Head (DH) with the HRBP. Calibration reviews proposed ratings for consistency across teams. The RM communicates the final rating to the employee after calibration.

## 6. Assessment

### 6.1 Weighting

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Achievement of goals | 70% |
| Competencies (Delivery, Collaboration, Client Focus, Growth) | 30% |

### 6.2 Inputs to the Growth competency

The Growth competency considers learning activity, including progress against the 40-hour annual learning target in NX-HR-006.

### 6.3 Other inputs

The RM also takes into account:

- non-completion of mandatory training (NX-GOV-002 §14.3);
- repeated non-compliance with office attendance requirements (NX-HR-003 §4.6);
- feedback from at least two colleagues or clients, collected in NexPeople, for employees at L4 and above.

## 7. Eligibility to Be Rated

### 7.1 New joiners

Employees who join on or before 31 December are rated for the cycle in which they joined. Employees who join between 1 January and 31 March are marked "New joiner – not rated" and receive their first rating at the end of the following cycle.

### 7.2 Parental leave

An employee absent on parental leave under NX-HR-007 for more than half of the cycle receives their previous cycle's rating. If they have no previous rating, they receive Meets.

### 7.3 Other long absences

An employee absent for more than six months of the cycle for any other reason, including Leave Without Pay, is marked "Not rated – extended absence".

### 7.4 Employees serving notice

An employee whose last working day falls before ratings are released is not rated for that cycle.

## 8. Promotion

### 8.1 Promotion cycle

Promotions are decided once a year after ratings are released and take effect on 1 July.

### 8.2 Eligibility

An employee is eligible for promotion consideration only if, on 1 July of the promotion year, they meet all of the following conditions:

1. They are confirmed (not on probation; NX-HR-001 §5.5).
2. They meet the minimum time in their current level in Table 8.2.
3. Their rating for the cycle just ended is Exceeds or Outstanding, and their rating for the previous cycle is at least Meets.
4. They are not on a Performance Improvement Plan (Section 9).
5. They have had no Medium or High severity finding under NX-GOV-002 §13.3 in the previous 12 months.

**Table 8.2 – Minimum time in current level**

| Promotion | Minimum time in current level |
|---|---|
| L1 → L2 | 12 months |
| L2 → L3 | 18 months |
| L3 → L4 | 24 months |
| L4 → L5 | 24 months |
| L5 → L6 | 36 months |
| L6 → L7 | 36 months |
| L7 → L8 | No minimum; at the CEO's discretion |

An employee who was "New joiner – not rated" in the previous cycle meets condition 3 if their rating for the cycle just ended is Exceeds or Outstanding.

### 8.3 Accelerated promotion

An employee rated Outstanding in each of the two most recent cycles may be considered with six months less than the minimum time in Table 8.2. Accelerated promotion does not apply to promotions from L6 to L7.

*Example:* An L2 Software Engineer who has been at L2 for 12 months on 1 July, and was rated Outstanding in the last two cycles, may be considered for L3, because the 18-month minimum is reduced to 12 months.

### 8.4 Nomination and approval

1. The RM nominates the employee in NexPeople during calibration.
2. The DH reviews and endorses the nomination.
3. The promotion is approved by the authority specified in NX-FIN-003 §4.
4. The employee receives a promotion letter in NexPeople before 1 July.

### 8.5 Off-cycle promotions

An off-cycle promotion may be made only to fill an approved vacancy at a higher level, with the approval of the Head of HR in addition to the approval in Section 8.4. The minimum time in level still applies.

### 8.6 Change of track at L5

Moving between the Engineering Manager and Principal Engineer tracks at L5 (NX-HR-001 §4.2) is a role change, not a promotion. It does not require the conditions in Section 8.2 and is approved by the DH.

## 9. Performance Improvement Plan

### 9.1 When a PIP is used

A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is put in place when:

- an employee receives a final rating of Does Not Meet; or
- an employee receives Partially Meets in two consecutive cycles; or
- during a cycle, the RM documents serious performance gaps and the HRBP agrees that a PIP is needed.

### 9.2 Duration

A PIP lasts 60 calendar days. It may be extended once by up to 30 calendar days, with the HRBP's agreement.

### 9.3 Content and check-ins

The PIP sets out specific, measurable objectives, the support Nexora will provide and the consequences of not meeting the objectives. The RM holds a check-in with the employee at least once a week and records it in NexPeople.

### 9.4 Outcomes

At the end of a PIP, the RM, with the HRBP, records one of three outcomes:

1. Successful completion: the PIP is closed.
2. Extension under Section 9.2, if not already extended.
3. Unsuccessful completion: employment may be terminated with notice or pay in lieu of notice under NX-HR-008.

### 9.5 Status during a PIP

A PIP is not probation under NX-HR-001 §5. An employee on a PIP remains a confirmed employee, and their notice period under NX-HR-008 does not change. During a PIP, the employee is not eligible for promotion, internal transfer or learning sponsorship above ₹50,000 under NX-HR-006.

## 10. Rating Appeals

An employee who disagrees with their final rating may appeal under NX-GOV-003 §7.4. The appeal starts at Level 2 of the grievance procedure and must be raised within 15 working days of the rating being released in NexPeople. Promotion decisions cannot be appealed separately from the rating.

## 11. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee | Sets goals, completes self-assessment, seeks feedback |
| Reporting Manager | Agrees goals, holds check-ins, proposes ratings, nominates for promotion, manages PIPs |
| Department Head | Chairs calibration, endorses promotions |
| HRBP | Supports calibration, ensures consistent application, agrees PIPs |
| Head of HR | Owns this Policy; approves distribution exceptions and off-cycle promotions |
