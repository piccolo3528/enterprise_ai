## 1. Welcome and Purpose of this Handbook

### 1.1 Purpose

This Handbook introduces Nexora Technologies Private Limited ("Nexora") and sets out the terms that apply to everyone employed by Nexora. It is the canonical source for employment categories, the job level framework, probation and working hours (see NX-GOV-001 §10). For all other subjects, it summarises the position and refers to the policy that governs it.

### 1.2 How to use this Handbook

Where this Handbook summarises another policy, the other policy prevails. If you find a difference between this Handbook and another current policy, follow the other policy and inform the HR Business Partner (HRBP) for your department.

## 2. About Nexora

### 2.1 Company overview

Nexora is a privately held IT services and software development company founded in 2011. It builds and maintains enterprise applications, cloud platforms and data solutions for clients in manufacturing, retail, logistics and financial services. Nexora employs approximately 500 people.

### 2.2 Locations

Nexora's registered and head office is in Hyderabad. Nexora also operates offices in Bengaluru and Pune. Office addresses, facilities and location-specific holidays are set out in NX-OPS-002.

## 3. Employment Categories

### 3.1 Categories

Nexora engages people in the following categories.

**Table 3.1 – Employment categories**

| Category | Description | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Full-time permanent | Employed on an open-ended contract | Open-ended |
| Fixed-term contract | Employed for a defined project or period | 6 to 24 months |
| Intern | Student engaged under an internship letter for learning purposes; receives a stipend | Up to 6 months |

### 3.2 Contractors and consultants

Contractors and consultants engaged through third-party vendors are not Nexora employees. They are not covered by this Handbook but must follow the Code of Conduct (NX-GOV-002) and the IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy (NX-IT-001) while working on Nexora premises or systems.

### 3.3 Benefit eligibility by category

Unless a policy states otherwise, full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees have the same entitlements. Interns are covered only by the terms of their internship letter, the Code of Conduct and the IT policies. Each policy states its own eligibility.

### 3.4 Conversion

A fixed-term contract employee who is converted to full-time permanent employment does not serve a new probation period if they have already completed probation under their fixed-term contract. An intern who joins as an employee serves the full probation period in Section 5.

## 4. Job Level Framework

### 4.1 Levels

Every employee holds one of eight job levels. The level determines entitlements and authorities across several policies, including notice periods (NX-HR-008), travel entitlements (NX-FIN-001), learning budgets (NX-HR-006) and approval authority (NX-FIN-003).

**Table 4.1 – Job levels**

| Level | Engineering track title | Business functions title | Band |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate Engineer | Associate | Foundation |
| L2 | Software Engineer | Analyst | Foundation |
| L3 | Senior Software Engineer | Senior Analyst | Professional |
| L4 | Lead Engineer | Lead | Professional |
| L5 | Engineering Manager / Principal Engineer | Manager | Management |
| L6 | Senior Engineering Manager | Senior Manager | Management |
| L7 | Director | Director | Leadership |
| L8 | Vice President | Vice President | Leadership |

### 4.2 Individual contributor path at L5

At L5, engineers may follow either the management path (Engineering Manager) or the individual contributor path (Principal Engineer). Both titles carry the same level and the same entitlements. A Principal Engineer is not required to manage direct reports.

### 4.3 Level changes

Promotions to a higher level follow NX-HR-005. A level is never reduced except as a documented outcome of a disciplinary process under NX-GOV-002 §13 or by written agreement with the employee.

### 4.4 Level in documents and systems

An employee's level is recorded in NexPeople and printed on their offer letter. Where a policy refers to "Leadership band", it means L7 and L8; "Management band" means L5 and L6.

## 5. Probation

### 5.1 Duration

All new employees serve a probation period of six months from their date of joining. Employees hired laterally directly into L7 or L8 serve a probation period of three months.

### 5.2 Confirmation review

The Reporting Manager (RM) must complete a confirmation review in NexPeople no later than 15 calendar days before the probation end date. The review has three possible outcomes:

1. Confirm the employee.
2. Extend probation (Section 5.3).
3. Recommend that employment ends, with the concurrence of the HRBP.

### 5.3 Extension

Probation may be extended only once, by up to three months, and only with the written concurrence of the HRBP. The RM must record specific reasons and improvement expectations. The total probation period may not exceed nine months, or six months for L7–L8 lateral hires.

### 5.4 Deemed confirmation

If the RM has not completed the confirmation review by the probation end date, the employee is deemed confirmed with effect from the day after the probation end date.

*Example:* An L2 Software Engineer joined on 5 January 2026. Her probation ends on 4 July 2026, and her RM's review was due by 19 June 2026. If no review is recorded by 4 July 2026, she is deemed confirmed from 5 July 2026.

### 5.5 Conditions during probation

During probation, an employee:

- is subject to the probation notice period in NX-HR-008 §4;
- is not eligible to apply for internal job transfers;
- is not eligible for promotion consideration under NX-HR-005;
- may take leave as provided in NX-HR-002.

### 5.6 Transfers and re-hires

An internal transfer between departments or locations does not restart probation. A former employee re-hired within 12 months of their last working day, at the same or a lower level, does not serve probation again if they had been confirmed during their previous employment.

## 6. Working Hours and Attendance

### 6.1 Standard working week

The standard working week is Monday to Friday. The standard working day is nine hours, including a 60-minute break.

### 6.2 Flexible start and core hours

Employees may start work at any time between 08:00 and 11:00 IST. All employees must be available between 11:00 and 16:00 IST (core hours), unless they are on approved leave or their client engagement requires a different schedule agreed in writing by the Department Head.

### 6.3 Attendance recording

Office attendance is recorded by access badge. Remote working days must be recorded in NexPeople in line with NX-HR-003. Where your badge record and NexPeople entries do not match for a day, NexPeople flags the day for correction by your RM within five working days.

### 6.4 Client-aligned schedules

Employees working on client engagements in other time zones may follow a shifted schedule. The shifted schedule must still include five hours of overlap with Indian core hours, or a documented alternative approved by the Department Head.

### 6.5 Night shift allowance

Employees in IT and Operations support teams who work an approved night shift, meaning a shift starting at or after 20:00 IST, receive a night shift allowance of ₹500 per shift, paid with the following month's salary. Night shifts ending after 22:00 IST include a company-arranged cab drop (NX-OPS-002).

### 6.6 Work on weekends and public holidays

Work on a weekend or public holiday requires prior RM approval. Compensatory off arising from such work is governed by NX-HR-002.

## 7. Pay and Benefits Overview

### 7.1 Salary payment

Salary is credited to the employee's registered bank account on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available in NexPeople by the third working day of the following month.

### 7.2 Insurance benefits

**Table 7.2 – Insurance cover**

| Benefit | Cover |
|---|---|
| Group Medical Insurance | ₹5,00,000 family floater per year covering the employee, spouse and up to two children |
| Parents / parents-in-law add-on | Optional; premium deducted from salary at the employee's cost |
| Group Term Life Insurance | Three times the employee's annual fixed pay |
| Group Personal Accident Insurance | ₹25,00,000 |

Insurance cover starts on the date of joining. Dependants must be enrolled in NexPeople within 30 calendar days of joining or of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth or adoption). Dependants enrolled late are added at the next policy renewal on 1 April.

### 7.3 Meals and transport

Each office has a cafeteria with subsidised meals. Nexora does not provide routine transport to or from the office. The only exception is the cab drop for night shifts ending after 22:00 IST (Section 6.5).

### 7.4 Leave, travel and expenses

Leave entitlements are set out in NX-HR-002 and NX-HR-007. Business travel and reimbursement are governed by NX-FIN-001 and NX-FIN-002.

## 8. Employee Referral Programme

### 8.1 Referral bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus.

**Table 8.1 – Referral bonus**

| Level of the hired candidate | Bonus |
|---|---|
| L1–L3 | ₹30,000 |
| L4 and above | ₹60,000 |

### 8.2 Payment

The bonus is paid with the salary for the month after the referred candidate completes probation. If either the referrer or the candidate leaves Nexora before then, no bonus is paid.

### 8.3 Exclusions

The following employees are not eligible for a referral bonus:

- employees in Human Resources and Talent Acquisition;
- the hiring manager for the position;
- any employee at L7 or L8;
- referrers of candidates who previously worked at Nexora and left less than 12 months before re-joining.

### 8.4 Submission

Referrals must be submitted through the Referrals section of NexPeople before the candidate's first interview. A referral submitted after the candidate has already applied directly is not eligible.

## 9. Outside Employment

### 9.1 Prior approval

Employees may not take up outside employment, paid consulting or freelance work without prior written approval from their Department Head and HRBP.

### 9.2 Prohibited activities

Approval is never granted for work for a Nexora client, a Nexora competitor, or any organisation that would require the use of Nexora information or equipment.

### 9.3 Activities that need only declaration

Unpaid contributions to open-source projects, occasional guest teaching and writing are permitted without prior approval. They must be declared under the conflict of interest process in NX-GOV-002 §5 and must not use Nexora confidential information.

## 10. Conduct and Dress

### 10.1 Code of Conduct

All employees must follow the Code of Conduct (NX-GOV-002). The Code prevails over this Handbook on matters of ethical conduct.

### 10.2 Dress code

Business casual is the standard dress code in all offices. Formal business attire is expected for client meetings and client-site visits unless the client's dress code differs.

## 12. Raising Concerns

Nexora provides separate channels for different types of concern:

- workplace grievances (workload, assignments, performance rating appeals, interpersonal disputes): NX-GOV-003;
- ethical concerns, fraud or bribery: the Ethics Hotline under NX-GOV-002 §12;
- sexual harassment: the Internal Committee under NX-GOV-002 §4.2;
- IT security incidents: NexDesk, as set out in NX-IT-001.

## 13. Personal Data and Employee Records

### 13.1 Keeping records up to date

Employees must update changes to their address, contact number, marital status, emergency contact and nominee details in NexPeople within 15 calendar days of the change.

### 13.2 Access to your records

Employees can view their own records in NexPeople. Requests for copies of documents not available in NexPeople are raised with the HRBP and fulfilled within 10 working days.

### 13.3 Handling of employee data

Employee personal data is classified and handled in accordance with NX-IT-002.
