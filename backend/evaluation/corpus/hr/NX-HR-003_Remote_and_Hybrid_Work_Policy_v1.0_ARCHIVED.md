## 1. Purpose

This Policy introduces hybrid working at Nexora following the return to offices, and sets out how employees may combine office and home working.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees. Interns work from the office on all working days.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Work from home (WFH) day | A working day on which the employee works from their registered home address |
| Office day | A working day on which the employee works from a Nexora office |

## 4. Hybrid Working Arrangement

### 4.1 Remote days

Employees may work from home for up to three days per week. The remaining working days are office days.

### 4.2 Choice of days

Employees agree their WFH days with their Reporting Manager. There are no fixed days on which office attendance is mandatory for all employees.

### 4.3 Team events

A Reporting Manager may require attendance at the office on a specific day for a team event, client visit or planning session by giving at least two working days' notice.

### 4.4 Unused WFH days

WFH days not used in a week do not carry over to the following week.

## 5. Working from Home

### 5.1 Location

WFH days must be worked from the employee's registered home address in NexPeople. Working from any other address requires the prior approval of the Reporting Manager. Working from outside India is not permitted.

### 5.2 Availability

Employees must be available during core hours (NX-HR-001) and reachable on Nexora's collaboration tools.

### 5.3 Recording

WFH days must be recorded in NexPeople on the day.

### 5.4 Equipment and security

Only Nexora-issued laptops may be used. Employees must follow NX-IT-001 when working from home.

## 6. New Joiners

New employees work from the office on every working day for the first two weeks after joining.

## 7. Internet Allowance

Employees who work from home under this Policy receive an internet allowance of ₹1,000 per month with their salary.

## 8. WFH and Leave

A WFH day is a working day and is not leave. Employees who are unwell must apply for leave under NX-HR-002.
