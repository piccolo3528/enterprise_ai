## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out Nexora's hybrid working model, the minimum office attendance expected of employees, and the conditions for working remotely. It is the canonical source for remote and hybrid attendance requirements (NX-GOV-001 §10).

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Employees covered

This Policy applies to full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees at all levels and locations.

### 2.2 Interns

Interns work from the office on all working days.

### 2.3 Employees deployed at client sites

Employees deployed full-time at a client site follow the client's attendance requirements. Section 4.4 explains how client-site days count.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Office day | A working day on which the employee works from any Nexora office or an approved client site |
| Anchor day | A day of the week on which office attendance is mandatory for all hybrid employees |
| Remote day | A working day on which the employee works from their registered home address |
| Work from anywhere (WFA) | Working from a location in India other than the registered home address or a Nexora office (Section 5) |
| Fully remote arrangement | An approved arrangement exempting an employee from minimum office attendance (Section 6) |

## 4. Attendance Requirements

### 4.1 Minimum office days

Hybrid employees must work from the office for a minimum of three days in each working week.

### 4.2 Anchor days

Tuesday and Wednesday are anchor days. Every hybrid employee must work from the office on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. The third office day is chosen by the employee in agreement with their Reporting Manager.

### 4.3 Weeks with leave or holidays

For each working day in a week on which the employee is on approved leave or which is a public holiday at their office location, the minimum number of office days for that week is reduced by one. The anchor-day requirement continues to apply on any anchor day on which the employee is working.

*Example:* A week has a public holiday on Monday and the employee takes Annual Leave on Friday. The minimum is reduced from three office days to one. Because the employee is working on Tuesday and Wednesday, both anchor days, she must still attend the office on both days.

### 4.4 What counts as an office day

The following count as office days:

- a day worked from any Nexora office, including an office other than the employee's base location;
- a day worked at a client site on approved client work;
- a day of approved business travel.

A day on which the employee badges into the office for less than four hours does not count as an office day.

### 4.5 Measurement

Compliance is measured monthly from access badge records, client-site timesheets and approved travel in NexPeople. NexPeople shows each employee and Reporting Manager the employee's compliance at the end of each calendar month.

### 4.6 Non-compliance

| Months below the minimum (consecutive) | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Automated reminder in NexPeople |
| 2 | Discussion with the Reporting Manager, recorded in NexPeople |
| 3 or more | Referral to the HRBP; non-compliance is recorded and taken into account in the annual performance review under NX-HR-005 |

## 5. Work From Anywhere

### 5.1 Allowance

Hybrid employees may work from anywhere in India for up to 15 working days per calendar year. Unused WFA days lapse on 31 December.

### 5.2 Conditions

- WFA may be taken in blocks of no more than 10 consecutive working days.
- WFA requires Reporting Manager approval in NexPeople at least seven calendar days in advance.
- Employees on probation are not eligible for WFA.
- WFA days are exempt from the office attendance requirement in Section 4, including anchor days.
- WFA from outside India is not permitted. Working remotely from outside India is permitted only during approved business travel.

### 5.3 Working conditions during WFA

During WFA, employees must work core hours (NX-HR-001 §6.2), use a secure network as required by NX-IT-001, and must not access information classified Restricted under NX-IT-002 from public or shared networks.

## 6. Fully Remote Arrangements

### 6.1 Eligibility

A fully remote arrangement may be approved in the following circumstances:

- a medical condition of the employee, supported by a medical certificate;
- the employee being the primary carer of a family member with a serious illness or disability;
- a role whose team, clients and responsibilities are entirely located outside the employee's base city.

### 6.2 Approval and review

A fully remote arrangement requires the joint approval of the Department Head and the Head of HR. It is approved for up to six months at a time and reviewed before each renewal.

### 6.3 Office attendance

Employees on a fully remote arrangement attend the office when required for specific business events, with at least five working days' notice. Nexora reimburses travel costs for such attendance under NX-FIN-001 if the employee lives outside their base city.

## 7. Other Exceptions to Minimum Office Attendance

**Table 7.1 – Exceptions**

| Situation | Exception | Governing document |
|---|---|---|
| Return from Maternity, Adoption or Surrogacy Leave | Fully remote for up to 12 weeks, by agreement with the Reporting Manager | NX-HR-007 §7.1 |
| Business continuity declaration requiring remote work | Office attendance suspended for the period declared | NX-OPS-001 |
| Temporary medical restriction | Up to 4 weeks remote on a medical certificate, approved by the HRBP | This Policy |
| Notice period with garden leave | No office attendance required | NX-HR-008 |

## 8. New Joiners

New employees work from the office on every working day for the first four weeks after joining, so that they can complete onboarding under NX-HR-004. The minimum office attendance requirement in Section 4 applies from week five.

## 9. Working Remotely

### 9.1 Location

Remote days are worked from the employee's registered home address in NexPeople, unless the day is a WFA day.

### 9.2 Availability

Employees working remotely must be available during core hours and reachable on Nexora's collaboration tools, with their camera available for scheduled meetings.

### 9.3 Recording

Remote days and WFA days must be recorded in NexPeople on the day they are worked.

### 9.4 Equipment and security

Only Nexora-issued devices (NX-IT-003) may be used for Nexora work. Employees must follow NX-IT-001 and NX-IT-002 when working remotely, including the rules on screen privacy and printing.

## 10. Allowances

**Table 10.1 – Remote work allowances**

| Allowance | Amount | Eligible employees |
|---|---|---|
| Internet allowance – hybrid | ₹1,000 per month | Hybrid employees |
| Internet allowance – fully remote | ₹1,500 per month | Employees on an approved fully remote arrangement |
| Home office set-up | ₹5,000, one time | Employees on an approved fully remote arrangement |

Internet allowances are paid with the monthly salary. The home office set-up allowance is claimed once through NexClaim within 60 days of the fully remote arrangement being approved.

## 11. Remote Work and Leave

A remote day or WFA day is a working day and is not leave. An employee who is unwell must apply for Sick Leave under NX-HR-002 rather than record a remote day.

## 12. Business Continuity

During a business continuity event, the Business Continuity Team may require all or some employees to work remotely under NX-OPS-001. Such a requirement overrides the attendance requirements in Section 4.
