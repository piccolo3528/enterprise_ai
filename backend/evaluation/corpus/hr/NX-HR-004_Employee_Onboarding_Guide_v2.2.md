## 1. Purpose

This Guide walks new employees, their Reporting Managers (RMs) and buddies through the steps from offer acceptance to the end of the first 90 days. It is a guide under NX-GOV-001 §4: it explains requirements set by other policies and creates no new entitlements. Where this Guide summarises a policy, the policy prevails.

## 2. Who This Guide Is For

| Audience | Sections most relevant |
|---|---|
| New full-time permanent and fixed-term employees | All sections |
| Interns | Section 11 |
| Lateral hires at L7 and L8 | Section 12 |
| Reporting Managers | Sections 3.4, 6, 7 and 9 |
| Buddies | Section 8 |

## 3. Before Your First Day

### 3.1 Pre-joining portal

After you accept your offer, you receive access to the NexPeople pre-joining portal. Upload the following documents at least five working days before your date of joining:

- government-issued photo identity proof and PAN card;
- educational certificates for your highest qualification;
- relieving letters or proof of resignation from your last two employers, where applicable;
- bank account details for salary payment;
- a passport-size photograph for your access badge.

### 3.2 Background verification

Nexora verifies your identity, address, education and employment with your last two employers. Verification starts after you accept your offer. Your employment is conditional on satisfactory verification, which is normally completed within 45 calendar days of joining. Delays in verification do not delay your start date.

### 3.3 Your base location

Your offer letter states your base office: Hyderabad, Bengaluru or Pune. Office addresses, reception hours and facilities are listed in NX-OPS-002.

### 3.4 Actions for the Reporting Manager

| Action | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Raise a laptop and access request in NexDesk (NX-IT-003) | At least 10 working days before the date of joining |
| Nominate a buddy (Section 8) | At least 5 working days before the date of joining |
| Prepare a 30-60-90 day plan (Section 6) | By the date of joining |

## 4. Your First Day

### 4.1 Arrival

Report to the reception of your base office at 09:30 IST on your date of joining. Bring the original identity document you uploaded.

### 4.2 Day 1 activities

1. Collect your temporary access badge from Facilities. Your permanent badge is issued within three working days.
2. Attend the induction session run by Human Resources (09:45–13:00), covering Nexora's history, departments, values and key policies.
3. Collect your laptop from IT and sign the asset acknowledgement in NexDesk (NX-IT-003).
4. Log in to your Nexora account and set up multi-factor authentication, as required by NX-IT-001.
5. Meet your Reporting Manager and buddy.

## 5. Your First 30 Days: Mandatory Checklist

The following items are mandatory. NexPeople tracks each item and reminds you and your RM before the deadline.

**Table 5.1 – Mandatory onboarding checklist**

| Item | Deadline (from date of joining) | Governing document |
|---|---|---|
| Information Security Awareness training in NexLearn | 7 calendar days | NX-IT-001 |
| Acknowledge the Employee Handbook and IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy in NexPeople | 15 calendar days | NX-HR-001, NX-IT-001 |
| Conflict of interest declaration (even if nothing to declare) | 15 calendar days | NX-GOV-002 §5.2 |
| Code of Conduct e-learning and acknowledgement | 30 calendar days | NX-GOV-002 §14.1 |
| Information Classification and Data Handling module | 30 calendar days | NX-IT-002 |
| Prevention of Sexual Harassment awareness module | 30 calendar days | NX-GOV-002 §4.2 |
| Enrol dependants for Group Medical Insurance | 30 calendar days | NX-HR-001 §7.2 |
| Set performance goals in NexPeople with your RM | 30 calendar days | NX-HR-005 |

### 5.1 Access to client systems

Access to client systems and client data is granted only after you complete Information Security Awareness training. If you miss the seven-day deadline, client access requests are held until you complete it.

### 5.2 Late dependant enrolment

Dependants not enrolled within 30 calendar days can be added only at the next insurance renewal on 1 April (NX-HR-001 §7.2).

### 5.3 Office attendance in your first four weeks

You work from the office on every working day for your first four weeks. The hybrid minimum of three office days a week applies from week five (NX-HR-003 §8).

## 6. Your First 90 Days

### 6.1 The 30-60-90 day plan

Your RM shares a 30-60-90 day plan on your first day. A typical plan looks like this:

| Period | Focus |
|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Complete mandatory training, learn the team's tools and processes, shadow colleagues, set goals |
| Days 31–60 | Contribute to project work with support, complete role-specific learning |
| Days 61–90 | Work independently on assigned tasks; agree development areas |

### 6.2 Check-ins

Your RM holds structured check-ins with you at 30, 60 and 90 days and records a short summary of each in NexPeople. Your HRBP meets you around day 45 to hear how your onboarding is going.

## 7. Probation

All new employees serve probation as set out in NX-HR-001 §5. Your RM completes your confirmation review in NexPeople before your probation ends. Your 30-, 60- and 90-day check-ins form part of the evidence for that review.

## 8. Buddy Programme

### 8.1 Role of the buddy

Your buddy is a colleague who helps you settle in, answers everyday questions and introduces you to people. Your buddy is not your RM and does not assess your performance.

### 8.2 Who can be a buddy

A buddy must be at L2 or above, confirmed, and preferably in the same team and base location as the new joiner.

### 8.3 Buddy commitments

The buddy meets the new joiner at least once a week during the first month and at least once every two weeks for the following two months. The programme ends at day 90.

## 9. Access and Tools

### 9.1 Access provided on Day 1

You receive access to email, NexPeople, NexHub, NexLearn, NexClaim and NexDesk on your first day.

### 9.2 Project and client access

Your RM requests project and client access through NexDesk once your security training is complete (Section 5.1).

### 9.3 Administrative rights

Local administrator rights on laptops are not granted by default. Requests are handled under NX-IT-001.

## 10. Leave, Pay and Expenses in Your First Months

| Question | Where to find the answer |
|---|---|
| Can I take leave during my first month? | Yes, within the leave credited or accrued to you under the new-joiner rules in NX-HR-002. |
| When is salary paid? | On the last working day of each month (NX-HR-001 §7.1). |
| Can I claim expenses for travel on joining? | Only if your offer letter says so; claims follow NX-FIN-002. |
| When can I use my learning budget? | As set out in NX-HR-006 for employees on probation. |

## 11. Interns

Interns complete the Information Security Awareness training, the Code of Conduct module and the Prevention of Sexual Harassment module within the deadlines in Table 5.1. Insurance enrolment, conflict of interest declarations and goal setting in NexPeople do not apply to interns. Interns work from the office on every working day (NX-HR-003 §2.2) and are supported by the internship coordinator in Human Resources instead of an HRBP.

## 12. Lateral Hires at L7 and L8

Lateral hires at L7 and L8 follow this Guide with three differences:

- they serve a three-month probation (NX-HR-001 §5.1);
- they have a one-to-one onboarding meeting with the CEO within the first two weeks;
- their 30-60-90 day plan is prepared with the Head of HR instead of a buddy.
