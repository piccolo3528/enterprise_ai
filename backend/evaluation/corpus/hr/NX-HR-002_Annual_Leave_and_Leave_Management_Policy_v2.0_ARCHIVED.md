## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out the types of leave available to Nexora employees, how leave is earned and applied for, and how unused leave is carried forward or encashed.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility

This Policy applies to full-time permanent and fixed-term contract employees. Interns receive leave as stated in their internship letter. Parental leave is governed by NX-HR-007.

## 3. Leave Year and Leave Types

### 3.1 Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

### 3.2 Leave types

**Table 3.2 – Leave types (version 2.0)**

| Leave type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Earned Leave (EL) | Planned time off for rest, holidays and personal matters |
| Casual/Sick Leave (CSL) | A single combined balance for unplanned personal needs and illness |
| Compensatory Off | Time off in lieu of approved work on a weekend or public holiday |
| Restricted Holiday | Optional holidays chosen from the list in NX-OPS-002 |
| Bereavement Leave | Death of a family member |
| Leave Without Pay (LWP) | Unpaid leave after paid leave is exhausted |

## 4. Entitlements

### 4.1 Annual entitlement

**Table 4.1 – Entitlement per leave year (version 2.0)**

| Leave type | Days per year | Credit method |
|---|---|---|
| Earned Leave | 18 working days | Accrues at 1.5 days per month |
| Casual/Sick Leave | 10 working days | Credited in full on 1 January |
| Restricted Holiday | 2 days | Credited on 1 January |
| Bereavement Leave | 3 working days per occasion | On application |

### 4.2 New joiners

Employees joining during the year accrue EL from the month of joining. CSL is credited pro-rata, rounded to the nearest whole day.

## 5. Applying for Leave

### 5.1 Notice

EL of five or more consecutive working days must be applied for at least 10 calendar days in advance. EL of fewer than five days must be applied for at least two working days in advance. CSL is applied for on the day of absence or on return.

### 5.2 Approval

The Reporting Manager approves leave of up to 10 consecutive working days. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days also requires the approval of the Department Head.

### 5.3 Medical certificate

A medical certificate is required for CSL taken for illness for more than three consecutive working days.

## 6. Carry-Forward and Encashment

### 6.1 Year-end encashment

At the end of each leave year, an employee may encash up to 10 days of unused EL. Encashment is paid at the daily rate of basic salary with the January salary.

### 6.2 Carry-forward

After any encashment, up to 15 days of unused EL are carried forward to the next leave year. Any EL above 15 days lapses on 31 December.

*Example:* An employee has 28 days of unused EL on 31 December 2025. She encashes 10 days. Of the remaining 18 days, 15 are carried forward into 2026 and 3 lapse.

### 6.3 Other leave

CSL, Restricted Holidays and Compensatory Off are not carried forward or encashed.

### 6.4 Encashment at exit

On leaving Nexora, an employee's entire unused EL balance is encashed at the daily rate of basic salary.

## 7. Compensatory Off

An employee who works on a weekend or public holiday with the prior approval of their Reporting Manager earns one day of Compensatory Off for a full day's work. Compensatory Off must be used within 90 days of the day worked. Employees at L7 and L8 are not eligible.

## 8. Restricted Holidays

Employees may take two Restricted Holidays per leave year, chosen from the list published in NX-OPS-002. Unused Restricted Holidays lapse at the end of the leave year.

## 9. Leave During Special Circumstances

### 9.1 Public holidays and weekends

Public holidays and weekends that fall within a period of EL are not counted as leave.

### 9.2 Parental leave

Maternity, paternity and adoption leave are governed by NX-HR-007.

### 9.3 Leave during notice period

Leave during the notice period is governed by NX-HR-008.
