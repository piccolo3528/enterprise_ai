## 1. Purpose

This Code sets out the standards of behaviour Nexora expects from everyone who works for or with it. It ranks highest among Nexora documents (NX-GOV-001 §4). On matters of ethical conduct, it prevails over any other policy.

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to:

- all Nexora employees, in every employment category and at every level, including employees on probation and employees serving notice;
- interns;
- contractors and consultants while working on Nexora premises, on Nexora systems or on Nexora client engagements;
- members of Nexora's Board of Directors.

The Code applies at Nexora offices, client sites, company events, during business travel and online whenever a person is identifiable as representing Nexora.

## 3. Core Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Integrity | We are honest in our work, our records and our dealings with clients and colleagues. |
| Respect | We treat everyone with dignity, regardless of role, level, background or identity. |
| Ownership | We take responsibility for our decisions and raise problems early. |
| Client trust | We protect client information and deliver what we commit to. |

## 4. Respectful Workplace

### 4.1 Harassment and discrimination

Nexora prohibits harassment, bullying, intimidation and discrimination on any ground, including gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, caste, ethnicity, disability, age, marital status or pregnancy. This applies to conduct in person, in writing and on any communication platform.

### 4.2 Sexual harassment

Nexora has constituted an Internal Committee (IC) at each office location in accordance with applicable law. Complaints of sexual harassment are made in writing to the IC, normally within three months of the incident or the last incident in a series. The IC's procedure is separate from the grievance procedure in NX-GOV-003, and sexual harassment complaints must not be raised as grievances. IC contact details are displayed in each office and published on NexHub.

### 4.3 Alcohol and substances

Possession or consumption of alcohol on Nexora premises is prohibited, except at company events where the Head of HR has approved its service in advance. Possession or use of illegal drugs at any time while on Nexora business is prohibited.

### 4.4 Workplace safety

Employees must follow posted safety instructions, evacuation procedures and the instructions of floor wardens. Emergency procedures are governed by NX-OPS-001.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest

### 5.1 Definition

A conflict of interest arises when a personal interest, relationship or activity could influence, or could appear to influence, an employee's decisions on behalf of Nexora.

### 5.2 Declaration

Employees must declare any actual or potential conflict of interest in NexPeople within 15 calendar days of becoming aware of it. In addition, every employee must complete an annual declaration in NexPeople by 31 May each year, even if they have nothing to declare.

### 5.3 Relatives and close personal relationships

An employee must not directly supervise, assess the performance of, approve the expenses of, or take hiring decisions about a relative or a person with whom they have a close personal relationship. Where such a reporting line exists or arises, it must be declared and the HRBP will arrange an alternative reporting line within 30 calendar days.

### 5.4 Financial interests

Employees must declare any holding of more than 2% of the shares of a Nexora client, vendor or competitor, held by them or their immediate family.

### 5.5 Outside activities

Outside employment requires prior approval under NX-HR-001 §9. Unpaid open-source contributions, teaching and writing must be declared under this Section.

## 6. Gifts, Hospitality and Anti-Bribery

### 6.1 Accepting gifts

An employee may accept a gift from a client, vendor or other business contact only if:

- its market value does not exceed ₹2,500 per occasion; and
- the total value of gifts from the same giver does not exceed ₹10,000 in a calendar year; and
- it is not offered during a tender, contract negotiation or vendor evaluation in which the employee is involved.

### 6.2 Gift Register

Every gift with a market value above ₹1,000 must be recorded in the Gift Register on NexHub within seven calendar days of receipt, even if it is within the limits in Section 6.1.

### 6.3 Prohibited gifts

Cash, cash equivalents, gift cards, vouchers, loans and securities may never be accepted, regardless of value.

### 6.4 Gifts above the limit

If an employee receives a gift that exceeds the limits in Section 6.1 and cannot be returned without causing serious offence, the employee must surrender it to the PCO within seven calendar days. The PCO decides whether it is displayed, raffled for charity or returned.

### 6.5 Hospitality

Employees may accept business meals and event invitations that are reasonable, infrequent and directly connected with business. Travel or accommodation paid for by a client or vendor requires prior written approval from the Department Head.

### 6.6 Giving gifts

Gifts to clients may not exceed ₹3,000 in value per recipient per occasion. Client gifts are claimed through NexClaim under NX-FIN-002 and approved in accordance with NX-FIN-003. Gifts may never be given while a tender or contract negotiation with the recipient's organisation is open.

### 6.7 Government officials and bribery

No gift, hospitality or payment of any value may be offered to a government official. Bribes and facilitation payments are prohibited in all circumstances. An employee asked for a bribe or facilitation payment must refuse and report the request to the PCO within one working day.

### 6.8 No exceptions

No exception may be granted to this Section (NX-GOV-001 §12.4).

## 7. Confidentiality and Data Protection

### 7.1 Handling information

Employees must handle Nexora and client information according to its classification under NX-IT-002 and share it only with people who need it for their work.

### 7.2 Client information

Client information is used only for the engagement for which it was provided. It must not be copied to personal devices, personal email or unapproved tools, including unapproved generative AI services (NX-IT-001).

### 7.3 Obligations after exit

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends. Exit procedures for returning information and assets are set out in NX-HR-008 and NX-IT-003.

## 8. Use of Company Assets and Systems

Nexora devices, systems, accounts and facilities are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted in accordance with NX-IT-001. Assets are issued and returned under NX-IT-003. Employees are responsible for reasonable care of assets assigned to them.

## 9. Social Media and Public Communication

### 9.1 Speaking for Nexora

Only Corporate Communications and the CEO may make public statements on behalf of Nexora. Media enquiries must be forwarded to Corporate Communications without comment.

### 9.2 Personal social media

Employees may mention that they work at Nexora on personal social media. They must not:

- disclose client names, project details or any information classified Internal or above (NX-IT-002), unless the client has publicly approved it;
- post content that harasses or disparages colleagues, clients or competitors;
- present personal opinions as Nexora's position.

## 10. Accurate Records and Anti-Fraud

### 10.1 Accurate records

Timesheets, attendance records, expense claims, invoices and project reports must be complete and accurate.

### 10.2 Fraud

Falsifying any record, including submitting a false or inflated expense claim, recording attendance that did not occur, or misrepresenting work completed, is fraud and is treated as a High severity violation under Section 13.3.

## 11. Political and Community Activity

Employees may take part in political and community activity in their personal capacity and outside working hours. Nexora funds, premises, systems and branding must not be used for political purposes. Nexora makes no political contributions.

## 12. Reporting Concerns and Non-Retaliation

### 12.1 Reporting channels

Employees who become aware of a possible breach of this Code must report it through one of the following channels:

- their Reporting Manager or HRBP;
- the Ethics Hotline: email ethics@nexora-tech.example or internal extension 7000;
- the anonymous web form on NexHub, which the Ethics Committee monitors.

Sexual harassment is reported to the IC under Section 4.2. Workplace grievances that are not breaches of this Code are raised under NX-GOV-003.

### 12.2 Anonymous reports

Anonymous reports are accepted through the web form. They are investigated where they contain enough information to do so.

### 12.3 Non-retaliation

Nexora prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is a High severity violation.

### 12.4 Knowingly false reports

Making a report that the reporter knows to be false is a Medium or High severity violation, depending on its impact.

## 13. Investigation and Disciplinary Process

### 13.1 Ethics Committee

The Ethics Committee consists of the Head of Compliance (chair), the Head of HR and the CFO. A member who is connected with a case is replaced by a nominee of the CEO for that case.

### 13.2 Timelines

- Preliminary assessment: within 5 working days of receiving a report.
- Investigation: completed within 30 calendar days of the preliminary assessment. The chair may extend this once by up to 15 calendar days, with written reasons.
- Outcome: communicated in writing to the person investigated within 5 working days of the Committee's decision.

### 13.3 Violation severity

**Table 13.3 – Violation severity and consequences**

| Severity | Examples | Possible consequences |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Late conflict of interest declaration; failure to record a permitted gift in the Gift Register; minor misuse of company equipment | Verbal or written counselling; mandatory refresher training |
| Medium | Repeated Low severity violations; accepting a gift above the limit without surrendering it; sharing Internal information on social media; disrespectful conduct towards a colleague | Written warning; ineligibility for promotion in the current review cycle; recovery of any loss |
| High | Fraud, including false expense claims; bribery; harassment; retaliation; disclosure of Confidential or Restricted information; violence or threats | Suspension pending investigation; termination of employment; recovery of loss; referral to authorities |

### 13.4 Suspension

An employee under investigation for a High severity violation may be suspended on full pay while the investigation is completed.

### 13.5 Appeals

An employee may appeal an Ethics Committee decision in writing to the CEO within 10 working days of receiving it. The CEO's decision is final. Code of Conduct decisions cannot be appealed through the grievance procedure in NX-GOV-003.

## 14. Training and Acknowledgement

### 14.1 New joiners

New employees must complete the Code of Conduct e-learning module in NexLearn and acknowledge this Code in NexPeople within 30 calendar days of joining (see NX-HR-004).

### 14.2 Annual refresher

All employees must complete the annual refresher module by 31 March each year.

### 14.3 Non-completion

Non-completion of training is recorded in NexPeople and taken into account in the annual performance review under NX-HR-005.
