## 1. Purpose

This Procedure gives employees a fair, confidential and time-bound way to raise and resolve concerns about their work and working environment. It is the canonical source for grievance escalation levels (NX-GOV-001 §10).

## 2. Scope and Eligibility

### 2.1 Who may raise a grievance

Any Nexora employee may raise a grievance, including employees on probation and employees serving their notice period.

### 2.2 Interns and contractors

Interns raise concerns with the internship coordinator in Human Resources, who applies this Procedure where appropriate. Contractors raise concerns through their vendor organisation.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Grievance | A concern, problem or complaint an employee raises about their work, working conditions or working relationships. |
| Grievant | The employee who raises the grievance. |
| Respondent | The person whose actions or decisions the grievance concerns, if any. |
| Working day | Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays at the grievant's office location (NX-OPS-002). |
| GRC | Grievance Redressal Committee (Section 8). |

## 4. What Can and Cannot Be Raised

### 4.1 Matters within scope

Examples of matters that can be raised under this Procedure:

- workload, work allocation or project assignment;
- the application of a policy to the employee, such as a rejected leave request or an expense claim deduction;
- performance rating outcomes (Section 7.4);
- interpersonal conflicts that do not amount to harassment;
- working conditions, facilities or equipment;
- errors in salary payment, allowances or reimbursements.

### 4.2 Matters outside scope

**Table 4.2 – Matters handled by other channels**

| Matter | Correct channel |
|---|---|
| Sexual harassment | Internal Committee, NX-GOV-002 §4.2 |
| Fraud, bribery, or other breaches of the Code of Conduct | Ethics Hotline, NX-GOV-002 §12 |
| Appeals against Ethics Committee decisions | CEO, NX-GOV-002 §13.5 |
| IT security incidents | NexDesk, NX-IT-001 |
| Requests for a salary increase or a change to compensation | Not grievable; discussed with the Reporting Manager during the annual review |

Errors in the payment of an agreed salary or allowance are grievable. A request for a higher salary is not.

### 4.3 Group grievances

Two or more employees affected by the same issue may raise a joint grievance. Each employee must be named, and one employee is nominated as the point of contact.

## 5. Escalation Levels

A grievance moves through up to three levels. Each level has a fixed response time, measured in working days from the date the grievance reaches that level.

**Table 5.1 – Escalation levels**

| Level | Handled by | Acknowledgement | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Reporting Manager (RM) | Within 2 working days | Within 7 working days |
| Level 2 | HR Business Partner (HRBP) together with the Department Head (DH) | Within 2 working days | Within 10 working days |
| Level 3 | Grievance Redressal Committee (GRC) | Hearing held within 10 working days | Written decision within 15 working days |

The GRC's decision is final within Nexora.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Raising a grievance

1. Open **NexPeople → Grievances → Raise Grievance**.
2. Describe the facts, including dates and the people involved.
3. Attach any supporting evidence.
4. State the outcome you are seeking.
5. Submit. NexPeople assigns a reference in the format `GRV-YYYY-NNNN` and routes the grievance to the correct starting level (Section 7).

### 6.2 Time limit for raising a grievance

A grievance must be raised within 60 calendar days of the event it concerns, or of the last event in a continuing series. The HRBP may accept a grievance raised after 60 days where the employee shows reasonable cause, such as extended medical leave. No grievance is accepted more than 180 calendar days after the event.

### 6.3 Escalating to the next level

An employee who is not satisfied with the outcome at a level may escalate to the next level in NexPeople within 5 working days of receiving the outcome. If a level does not provide a resolution within its time limit in Table 5.1, the employee may escalate immediately without waiting for a decision.

### 6.4 Level 3 hearing

At the GRC hearing, the grievant may be accompanied by one Nexora employee who is not a party to the grievance. External representatives, including lawyers, are not permitted. The respondent, if any, is given an opportunity to respond.

### 6.5 Outcomes

Every outcome is recorded in NexPeople and communicated to the grievant in writing. The outcome states the reasons for the decision and, where a remedy is granted, the action and the date by which it will be completed.

## 7. Starting Level and Special Routes

### 7.1 Default route

A grievance starts at Level 1 unless one of the routes in Sections 7.2 to 7.5 applies.

### 7.2 Grievance concerning the Reporting Manager

A grievance about the actions or decisions of the employee's own RM starts at Level 2.

### 7.3 Grievance concerning the Department Head

A grievance about the employee's DH starts at Level 2, where it is handled by the HRBP together with the Head of HR instead of the DH.

### 7.4 Performance rating appeals

An employee who disagrees with their final annual performance rating (NX-HR-005) may appeal under this Procedure, subject to the following rules:

- the appeal starts at Level 2;
- it must be raised within 15 working days of the rating being released in NexPeople;
- only one appeal per review cycle is permitted;
- the Level 2 decision may be escalated to Level 3 under Section 6.3.

The 60-calendar-day time limit in Section 6.2 does not apply to rating appeals; the 15-working-day limit applies instead.

### 7.5 Employees serving notice

An employee serving notice should raise any grievance at least 10 working days before their last working day. A grievance raised later is still accepted, but its resolution may continue after the last working day. The full and final settlement under NX-HR-008 is not withheld while a grievance is pending.

### 7.6 Grievances concerning Human Resources

A grievance about an HRBP is handled at Level 2 by the Head of HR and the DH. A grievance about the Head of HR goes directly to Level 3, and the CEO nominates the GRC chair for that case.

## 8. Grievance Redressal Committee

### 8.1 Composition

The GRC consists of:

- the Head of HR (chair);
- one Department Head from a department not involved in the grievance;
- two employees at L5 or above, nominated annually by the Head of HR from different departments.

At least one GRC member must be a woman.

### 8.2 Quorum and conflicts

The quorum for a hearing is three members, including the chair or the chair's nominee. A member who is connected with the grievance must withdraw and is replaced by the Head of HR's nominee for that case.

## 9. Confidentiality and Records

Grievance information is shared only with the people handling the grievance and anyone who must take action on its outcome. Grievance records are classified Confidential under NX-IT-002 and retained for five years after the grievance is closed.

## 10. Non-Retaliation

No employee may be penalised for raising a grievance in good faith or for taking part in a grievance process. Retaliation is a High severity violation under NX-GOV-002 §13.3 and should be reported through the Ethics Hotline.

## 11. Anonymous Grievances

Grievances must be raised in the employee's own name, because resolving them usually requires discussion with the grievant. Employees who wish to remain anonymous should use the anonymous web form under NX-GOV-002 §12.2, which handles Code of Conduct concerns.

## 12. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Grievant | Raises the grievance promptly, provides accurate facts and attends meetings |
| Reporting Manager | Resolves Level 1 grievances within the time limits |
| HRBP | Routes grievances, resolves Level 2 grievances with the DH, decides on late grievances |
| Department Head | Resolves Level 2 grievances with the HRBP; implements agreed remedies |
| GRC | Hears and decides Level 3 grievances |
| Head of HR | Chairs the GRC; reports grievance statistics to the CEO each quarter |
