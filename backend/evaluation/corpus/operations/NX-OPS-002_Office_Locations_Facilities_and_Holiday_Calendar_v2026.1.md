## 1. Purpose

This document lists Nexora's offices and their facilities, classifies Indian cities into tiers for travel purposes, and publishes the holiday calendar for each office. It is the canonical source for travel city tiers and for public and restricted holidays (NX-GOV-001 §10). A new edition is published each calendar year under NX-GOV-001 §6.1.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to all employees, interns, contractors and visitors at Nexora offices, and to all employees for the purposes of the holiday calendar and city tiers.

## 4. City Tier Classification

### 4.1 Tiers

Domestic cities are classified into three tiers. The tiers determine hotel limits and per diem rates under NX-FIN-001 and any amendment to it in force.

**Table 4.1 – City tiers**

| Tier | Cities |
|---|---|
| Tier A | Mumbai; Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida); Bengaluru; Hyderabad; Chennai; Kolkata; Pune |
| Tier B | Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Coimbatore, Visakhapatnam, Indore, Bhubaneswar, Nagpur |
| Tier C | All other cities and towns in India |

### 4.2 Notes

- Inclusion of a city in Table 4.1 does not indicate that Nexora has an office there. Nexora's offices are listed in Table 3.1.
- Where a traveller stays in a suburb or satellite town of a listed city, the tier of the listed city applies if the stay is within 30 km of the city centre.
- Tier changes take effect only from the effective date of the edition that makes them. Travel completed before that date is assessed under the tiers in force at the time of travel (NX-GOV-001 §7.4).

## 5. Office Facilities

### 5.1 Office access

Employees can enter offices with their access badge between 07:00 and 23:00 IST on working days. Employees in approved IT and Operations support teams have 24-hour access. Access on weekends and public holidays requires prior approval from the Reporting Manager, notified to Facilities.

### 5.2 Visitors

Visitors must be registered in NexHub by their Nexora host at least one working day in advance. Visitors sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge and are escorted at all times. Visitors are not permitted in project areas designated for client work without the engagement lead's approval.

### 5.3 Cafeterias

Each office has a cafeteria serving subsidised breakfast, lunch and snacks on working days. The Hyderabad and Bengaluru cafeterias also serve dinner until 21:30 IST.

### 5.4 Creche

The Hyderabad office has an on-site creche for children aged six months to six years, open 08:00–19:00 IST on working days. Eligibility and the creche allowance for Bengaluru and Pune employees are set out in NX-HR-007 §8.

### 5.5 Parking

Two-wheeler parking is available free of charge at all offices. Car parking spaces are limited and are allocated by Facilities each quarter.

**Table 5.5 – Car parking spaces**

| Office | Car parking spaces | Allocation priority |
|---|---|---|
| Hyderabad | 80 | Employees at L6 and above; employees with a disability; carpools of three or more employees |
| Bengaluru | 40 | As above |
| Pune | 30 | As above |

### 5.6 Lockers

Personal lockers are available on request at all offices. Employees who are allocated a locker may leave their laptop in the office overnight (NX-IT-001 §6.6). Lockers must be emptied and keys returned by the last working day (NX-HR-008 §10).

### 5.7 Night shift cab drop

Facilities arranges a cab drop for employees whose approved night shift ends after 22:00 IST (NX-HR-001 §6.5). Cab requests must be submitted in NexHub by 18:00 IST on the day of the shift.

### 5.8 Meeting rooms

Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar system. Rooms in client-dedicated project areas are reserved for that client's project team.

### 5.9 Lost access badges

A lost access badge must be reported to Facilities and IT Security immediately. A replacement is issued at the recovery value in NX-IT-003 §8.

### 6.4 Holidays falling on weekends

Fixed public holidays that fall on a Saturday or Sunday are not moved to a working day and no substitute holiday is given. In 2026, Independence Day (all offices) falls on a Saturday and Kannada Rajyotsava (Bengaluru) falls on a Sunday.

### 6.5 Which calendar applies

Employees follow the calendar of their base office as recorded in NexPeople, including when travelling on business or working remotely. Employees deployed full-time at a client site follow the client's holiday calendar where the client contract requires it; otherwise they follow their base office calendar.

### 6.6 Changes to festival dates

Dates of festivals that follow the lunar calendar are based on the expected dates at the time of publication. If the date of a festival is officially changed, Human Resources announces the revised date on NexHub at least seven calendar days in advance where possible, and the revised date replaces the published date.

### 6.7 Work on a public holiday

Work on a public holiday requires prior Reporting Manager approval (NX-HR-001 §6.6). Compensatory off is governed by NX-HR-002.

## 7. Safety and Emergencies

Each office has trained floor wardens and first-aiders, whose names are displayed on every floor. Evacuation drills are held twice a year (NX-OPS-001 §10). In an emergency, employees follow the instructions of floor wardens and go to the assembly point shown on the floor plan. Business continuity arrangements are set out in NX-OPS-001.
